# Two-Week Lesson Plan: Artificial Intelligence Regulation

## Lesson Metadata
- Unit: Month 02 - Tech Governance and Regulation
- Lesson Number: 01-02
- Duration: 2 weeks
- Modality: Seminar, guided reading, structured discussion, and speaking tasks
- Audience: Arabic learners at ILR-3

## Objectives
- Discuss major issues in artificial intelligence regulation using Arabic suitable for ILR-3 analytic discussion.
- Compare competing regulatory models and explain their political, legal, economic, and security implications.
- Interpret assigned readings on AI governance and summarize key arguments with precision.
- Participate in sustained speaking tasks that require justification, comparison, rebuttal, and policy recommendation.

## Target Language Functions
- Defining and classifying policy problems
- Comparing regulatory approaches
- Explaining cause and effect
- Expressing qualified agreement and disagreement
- Supporting claims with evidence
- Recommending policy action

## Materials
- Instructor-selected Arabic-language article on AI regulation and state oversight.
- Arabic-language policy brief on balancing innovation and regulation.
- Short comparative reading on two different national or regional regulatory models.
- Vocabulary handout and discussion-preparation worksheet.

## Two-Week Sequence

### Week 1: Foundations of AI Regulation
#### Focus
Build topic vocabulary, establish the policy landscape, and practice summarizing regulatory arguments.

#### Reading Assignment
- Read one Arabic-language overview of AI regulation.
- Annotate terms related to oversight, compliance, transparency, and risk.
- Prepare a 3-5 sentence Arabic summary of the author's main argument.

#### Discussion Topics
- Why are governments moving to regulate artificial intelligence?
- What are the main risks associated with unregulated AI systems?
- How should policymakers balance innovation with public protection?
- Which sectors should face stricter AI oversight and why?

#### Speaking Activities
- Pair briefing: explain the purpose of AI regulation to a partner in Arabic.
- Small-group comparison: compare two regulatory priorities such as innovation and safety.
- Roundtable discussion: identify the most urgent AI policy challenge for governments.
- Short oral summary: present the main idea of the reading and defend its relevance.

#### Vocabulary Focus
- oversight
- compliance
- transparency
- accountability
- risk assessment
- public interest
- safeguards

### Week 2: Comparative Regulation and Policy Debate
#### Focus
Deepen analysis through comparison, policy evaluation, and extended speaking tasks.

#### Reading Assignment
- Read an Arabic-language policy brief comparing two AI regulatory approaches.
- Identify differences in enforcement, scope, and policy priorities.
- Prepare notes for a discussion on strengths, weaknesses, and likely outcomes.

#### Discussion Topics
- Which model is more effective: strict pre-approval regulation or flexible risk-based oversight?
- How do economic competitiveness and national security shape AI regulation?
- What unintended consequences can emerge from overregulation or weak regulation?
- Should international standards govern artificial intelligence, or should states regulate independently?

#### Speaking Activities
- Structured debate: one side argues for tighter regulation, the other for lighter oversight.
- Policy advisory role-play: brief a ministry on an AI governance proposal.
- Comparative presentation: explain which regulatory model is more sustainable and why.
- Follow-up question drill: respond to challenge questions that test precision and reasoning.

#### Vocabulary Focus
- licensing
- enforcement
- regulatory burden
- interoperability
- strategic competition
- ethical standards
- legal liability

## Assessment Evidence
- Participation in sustained Arabic discussion using ILR-3 level reasoning and topic control.
- Oral summaries that accurately capture the key points of assigned readings.
- Comparative speaking task evaluating two regulatory approaches.
- End-of-sequence policy recommendation delivered in Arabic with supporting justification.
