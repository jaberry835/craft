# Needs Analysis Summary

## Target Learners
Professional Arabic learners operating at or near ILR-3 expectations in analytic discussion.

## Target Use Cases
- Briefing and discussing policy developments
- Interpreting economic indicators and strategic implications
- Analyzing public health risks and state responses
- Comparing cross-sector developments in spoken and written Arabic

## Development Inputs
- Learner profile
- Mission/task analysis
- Source-domain vocabulary requirements
- Register and discourse expectations
