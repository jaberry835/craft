# AI Regulation Two-Week Facilitation Notes

## Instructional Goal
Support ILR-3 learners in sustaining analytic Arabic discussion on artificial intelligence regulation.

## Instructor Priorities
- Push learners beyond description into explanation and evaluation.
- Require evidence-based answers rather than short opinion statements.
- Recycle policy vocabulary across readings, summaries, and debate.
- Use follow-up questions to test precision, comparison, and justification.

## Suggested Follow-Up Questions
- What evidence supports that claim?
- How is that model different in practice?
- What tradeoff does that policy create?
- Which actor benefits or loses under this approach?
- What unintended outcome could emerge?

## Performance Look-Fors
- Sustained responses rather than isolated sentences.
- Clear comparison language.
- Policy reasoning with cause and effect.
- Topic-appropriate register and terminology.
