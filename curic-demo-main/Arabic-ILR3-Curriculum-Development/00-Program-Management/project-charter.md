# Project Charter

## Scope
Develop a 6-month ILR-3 Arabic curriculum for professional discussion and analysis in technology policy, economics, and public health.

## Core Deliverables
- Curriculum framework
- Syllabus and scope and sequence
- Monthly unit plans
- Lesson plans
- Student materials
- Instructor guides
- Assessments and rubrics
- Practical exercises and capstone materials
- QA and revision log
