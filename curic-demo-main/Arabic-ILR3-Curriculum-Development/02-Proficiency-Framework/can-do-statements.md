# ILR-3 Can-Do Statements

## Discussion
Learner can sustain extended discussion on complex professional topics, clarify nuance, and defend an interpretation.

## Briefing
Learner can deliver organized spoken summaries and analytic briefings on policy, economic, and public health developments.

## Reading
Learner can interpret authentic texts, identify argument structure, and extract relevant evidence.

## Writing
Learner can produce concise analytic responses using appropriate terminology and coherent reasoning.
