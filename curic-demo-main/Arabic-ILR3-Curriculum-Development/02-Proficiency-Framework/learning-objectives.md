# ILR-3 Learning Objectives

## Performance Frame
Learners should perform at ILR-3 in spoken Arabic by sustaining structured, precise, and analytically grounded discussion on complex professional topics. They should be able to explain developments, compare competing interpretations, support judgments with relevant detail, and respond to follow-up questions with adequate fluency and control.

## Learning Objectives

### Technology Regulation
- Discuss major technology regulation issues in Arabic, including state oversight, platform governance, data protection, and emerging technology controls.
- Explain the policy rationale behind different regulatory approaches and compare how governments frame security, innovation, and civil liberties concerns.
- Analyze the likely implications of regulatory decisions for domestic stability, regional influence, and international competition.
- Defend an evidence-based position on a technology policy question using organized reasoning, relevant examples, and appropriate professional register.

### Economic Trends
- Discuss current economic trends in Arabic, including inflation, unemployment, trade patterns, industrial policy, and market volatility.
- Interpret and summarize economic developments using cause-and-effect language, comparison, and trend analysis.
- Assess the political and strategic significance of economic shifts and explain how they may affect state behavior or public sentiment.
- Present and justify a reasoned judgment about an economic scenario while responding to questions, clarification requests, and alternative interpretations.

### Public Health Policy
- Discuss public health policy issues in Arabic, including system capacity, disease response, risk communication, vaccination policy, and emergency coordination.
- Explain how public health decisions are shaped by political, economic, and social constraints.
- Compare policy responses across cases and evaluate their likely effectiveness, tradeoffs, and second-order effects.
- Deliver a clear analytic discussion of a public health policy challenge using coherent argumentation, relevant terminology, and sustained professional discourse.

## ILR-3 Performance Indicators
- Sustain conversation and formal discussion on complex topics with only occasional difficulty.
- Organize ideas clearly across extended responses, briefings, and exchanges.
- Support claims with explanation, comparison, and relevant evidence rather than isolated opinion.
- Use topic-appropriate vocabulary and professional register with sufficient precision for analytic discussion.
- Handle complications, follow-up questions, and shifts in perspective without losing overall coherence.
