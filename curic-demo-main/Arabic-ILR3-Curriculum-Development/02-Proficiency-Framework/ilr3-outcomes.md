# ILR-3 Outcomes

## Target Performance
Learners should be able to discuss, explain, compare, hypothesize, and justify positions in Arabic across complex professional topics with sufficient control of register, precision, and discourse structure for sustained analytic interaction.

## Outcome Areas
- Oral discussion and debate
- Oral briefing and summarization
- Reading and interpreting policy and media texts
- Analytic writing and synthesis
- Domain-specific vocabulary deployment
