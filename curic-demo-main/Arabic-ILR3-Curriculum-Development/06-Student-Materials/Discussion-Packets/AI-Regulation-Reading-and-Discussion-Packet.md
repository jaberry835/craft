# AI Regulation Reading and Discussion Packet

## Week 1 Reading Assignment
Read an Arabic-language overview of AI regulation focused on state oversight, transparency, and accountability.

### Reading Tasks
- Identify the author's main claim.
- Note three arguments supporting regulation.
- Extract vocabulary related to compliance, risk, and public protection.
- Prepare a short Arabic summary of the text.

### Week 1 Discussion Questions
- Why do states regulate artificial intelligence?
- What risks are most urgent in the current policy environment?
- How can regulation protect the public without slowing innovation?
- Which institutions should lead AI oversight?

## Week 2 Reading Assignment
Read a comparative Arabic-language policy brief on two AI regulatory models.

### Reading Tasks
- Compare the scope of each model.
- Identify differences in enforcement and implementation.
- Note the economic and security assumptions behind each approach.
- Prepare to defend which model is more sustainable.

### Week 2 Discussion Questions
- Which regulatory model is more realistic for fast-moving technology?
- How should governments respond to bias, privacy, and security concerns?
- What are the costs of overregulation?
- Should global standards guide national AI policy?

## Speaking Preparation
- Prepare one 2-minute oral summary for each reading.
- Prepare one policy recommendation with supporting evidence.
- Prepare one rebuttal to an opposing viewpoint.
