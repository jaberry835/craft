# AI Regulation Vocabulary Handout

## Theme
Artificial Intelligence Regulation

## Core Terms
- artificial intelligence regulation - تنظيم الذكاء الاصطناعي
- algorithmic accountability - المساءلة الخوارزمية
- data governance - حوكمة البيانات
- privacy protection - حماية الخصوصية
- risk-based regulation - التنظيم القائم على المخاطر
- compliance framework - إطار الامتثال
- licensing regime - نظام الترخيص
- transparency requirement - متطلب الشفافية
- public oversight - الرقابة العامة
- enforcement mechanism - آلية الإنفاذ
- bias mitigation - الحد من التحيز
- national security concern - هاجس الأمن القومي
- innovation policy - سياسة الابتكار
- cross-border standards - المعايير العابرة للحدود
- ethical safeguards - الضمانات الأخلاقية

## Discussion Use
Use each term in an Arabic sentence that explains a policy concern, a regulatory tool, or a strategic tradeoff.

## Practice Prompts
- Which term is most important for public trust in AI systems?
- Which term is most difficult to implement across borders?
- Which term is most likely to create tension between innovation and control?
