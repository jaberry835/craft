# AI Regulation ILR-3 Assessment Rubric

## Scoring Bands
- 4: Exceeds ILR-3 target
- 3: Meets ILR-3 target
- 2: Approaching ILR-3 target
- 1: Below ILR-3 target

## 1. Vocabulary Mastery
### 4
Uses AI regulation terminology accurately, consistently, and flexibly in context.

### 3
Uses most key terms accurately and appropriately in context with only minor imprecision.

### 2
Uses some key terms correctly but shows inconsistency, limited range, or confusion between related concepts.

### 1
Shows weak control of essential terminology and frequent misuse that affects meaning.

## 2. Listening Comprehension
### 4
Identifies main ideas, key details, and implied policy concerns with strong accuracy.

### 3
Understands main ideas and most supporting details and can explain the central policy concern.

### 2
Understands only part of the spoken message and misses important detail or implications.

### 1
Shows limited understanding of the spoken passage and cannot reliably identify the main point.

## 3. Speaking Proficiency
### 4
Sustains clear, precise, and well-organized Arabic discussion with strong fluency, effective support, and confident handling of follow-up questions.

### 3
Sustains organized Arabic discussion on the topic with adequate fluency, relevant support, and generally effective responses to questions.

### 2
Can express a position but shows uneven control, limited development, or difficulty sustaining responses under pressure.

### 1
Provides fragmented or unclear responses and cannot sustain effective professional discussion on the topic.

## 4. Policy Analysis
### 4
Identifies the policy problem clearly, evaluates stakeholders and tradeoffs insightfully, and offers a well-supported recommendation.

### 3
Identifies the main policy problem, addresses key tradeoffs, and provides a reasonable recommendation with support.

### 2
Provides partial analysis with limited depth, incomplete tradeoff discussion, or weak recommendation support.

### 1
Shows minimal analytic development and does not clearly explain the policy issue or recommendation.

## Suggested Scoring Use
- 14-16: Strong ILR-3 performance
- 10-13: Meets ILR-3 target overall
- 7-9: Approaching ILR-3 target
- 4-6: Below ILR-3 target
