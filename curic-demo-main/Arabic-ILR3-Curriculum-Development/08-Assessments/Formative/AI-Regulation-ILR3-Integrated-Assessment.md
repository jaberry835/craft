# ILR-3 Integrated Assessment: AI Regulation

## Assessment Type
Formative integrated assessment

## Target Level
ILR-3 Arabic

## Purpose
Measure learner performance in four areas within a policy-focused Arabic task set:
- vocabulary mastery
- listening comprehension
- speaking proficiency
- policy analysis

## Assessment Scenario
The learner is participating in a professional policy environment focused on proposed artificial intelligence regulations. The learner must understand key terminology, process spoken input, analyze policy tradeoffs, and respond orally in clear, sustained Arabic.

## Assessment Components

### Part 1: Vocabulary Mastery
#### Task
The learner demonstrates command of core AI regulation terminology by:
- matching terms to definitions or policy functions
- using selected terms in short Arabic policy statements
- distinguishing between closely related concepts such as oversight, enforcement, transparency, and accountability

#### Evidence Collected
- accuracy of term recognition
- accuracy of usage in context
- appropriateness of terminology in policy discussion

### Part 2: Listening Comprehension
#### Task
The learner listens to a short Arabic briefing or panel excerpt on AI regulation and then:
- identifies the main argument
- notes key supporting points
- explains the speaker's policy concern
- identifies one tradeoff or implication raised in the audio

#### Evidence Collected
- comprehension of main ideas
- comprehension of supporting detail
- ability to infer policy implications from spoken input

### Part 3: Speaking Proficiency
#### Task
The learner delivers a 2-3 minute oral response in Arabic addressing a proposed AI regulation policy. The learner must:
- state a clear position
- justify the position with reasons and examples
- compare at least two policy options
- respond to follow-up questions from the instructor or panel

#### Evidence Collected
- clarity and coherence of spoken response
- fluency and control across sustained discourse
- effective response to challenge questions
- appropriate use of professional register

### Part 4: Policy Analysis
#### Task
The learner analyzes a short policy scenario involving AI regulation and explains:
- the main policy problem
- the relevant stakeholders
- the likely benefits and risks of the proposed policy
- a recommended course of action

This response may be delivered orally or in a short written analytic note, depending on instructional use.

#### Evidence Collected
- identification of core policy issues
- depth of analysis
- attention to tradeoffs and consequences
- quality of final recommendation

## Administration Guidance
- Total time: 35-45 minutes
- Vocabulary section: 8-10 minutes
- Listening section: 8-10 minutes
- Speaking section: 10-15 minutes
- Policy analysis section: 10 minutes

## Materials Needed
- AI regulation vocabulary list
- short Arabic audio briefing or recorded panel excerpt
- policy scenario prompt
- scoring rubric

## Performance Standard
A learner performing at ILR-3 should demonstrate the ability to sustain professional Arabic discussion on AI regulation with clear organization, accurate topic vocabulary, solid comprehension of spoken policy content, and analytic reasoning that moves beyond simple description.
