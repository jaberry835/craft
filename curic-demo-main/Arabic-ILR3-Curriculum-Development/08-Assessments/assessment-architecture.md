# Assessment Architecture

## Diagnostic
Entry benchmark for speaking, reading, and analytic discussion.

## Formative
Ongoing checks embedded in units and lessons.

## Midpoint
Formal checkpoint at the end of Month 3.

## Capstone
Integrated task assessing ILR-3 performance across the three focus areas.

## Rubrics
Shared analytic rubrics aligned to ILR-3 discourse expectations.
