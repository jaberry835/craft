# ILR-3 Speaking Exercise: Government Panel on AI Regulation

## Exercise Type
Role-play speaking exercise

## Scenario
A government panel is meeting to discuss proposed artificial intelligence regulations. The student participates as a policy advisor, regulatory official, or subject-matter specialist and must present a clear position on the proposed regulations, defend that position with reasoning and evidence, and respond to challenging follow-up questions from the panel.

## Target Level
ILR-3 Arabic

## Task Overview
The student will deliver an initial position statement in Arabic, participate in panel discussion, and answer challenge questions that test clarity, precision, and analytic control.

## Student Tasks
- Present a clear position on the proposed AI regulations.
- Defend the position using policy reasoning, comparison, and relevant examples.
- Answer challenging questions that test assumptions, tradeoffs, and possible consequences.
- Maintain coherent, professional Arabic throughout the exchange.

## Suggested Roles
- Ministry of Technology advisor
- National security policy analyst
- Data protection regulator
- Economic competitiveness advisor
- Civil society policy specialist

## Preparation Guidance
Before the exercise, the student should prepare:
- one main policy recommendation
- two supporting arguments
- one anticipated objection and response
- key terminology related to oversight, compliance, innovation, privacy, and risk

## Exercise Flow
### Part 1: Opening Position
The student gives a 2-3 minute Arabic statement explaining the recommended approach to AI regulation.

### Part 2: Defense of Position
Panel members ask follow-up questions about feasibility, enforcement, economic impact, civil liberties, innovation, and national security.

### Part 3: Challenge Round
The student answers more difficult questions that introduce counterarguments, tradeoffs, or alternative regulatory models.

### Part 4: Closing Recommendation
The student gives a brief closing statement summarizing the preferred policy approach and why it is the most effective.

## Sample Challenge Questions
- Why should the government regulate artificial intelligence now instead of waiting for further technological development?
- How would your proposal avoid harming innovation and investment?
- What evidence suggests that this regulatory model would actually improve public safety?
- How would you respond to critics who argue that the proposal gives the state too much control?
- If another country adopts a weaker regulatory approach, how should your government respond?
- What unintended consequences could emerge from your proposal?

## Language Expectations
The student should be able to:
- explain a position clearly
- compare policy options
- justify recommendations with supporting detail
- respond to pressure questions without losing coherence
- use professional, topic-appropriate vocabulary

## Evaluation Criteria
### 1. Position Clarity
- Presents a clear, arguable position from the beginning.
- Maintains consistency between the opening claim and later responses.

### 2. Analytic Reasoning
- Supports claims with explanation, comparison, and evidence.
- Addresses tradeoffs and second-order effects rather than giving simple opinion.

### 3. Response to Challenge Questions
- Answers directly and appropriately.
- Handles follow-up pressure without major breakdown in meaning or organization.

### 4. Language Control
- Uses Arabic with sufficient fluency and precision for sustained professional discussion.
- Demonstrates control of key vocabulary and discourse structures.

### 5. Organization and Coherence
- Organizes ideas logically across the full interaction.
- Uses transitions and framing language to guide the listener.

## Performance Standard
A strong ILR-3 performance sustains extended discussion on AI regulation with clear organization, relevant support, appropriate register, and effective responses to challenge questions, even when the discussion becomes more complex or adversarial.
