# DIRECTIVE 925 — Federal Consolidated Agency Brand Identity & Visual Standards

**Issuing Authority:** Public Affairs Office (PAO), Brand Standards Board (BSB)
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024
**Last Revised:** January 14, 2026
**Reference:** FCA Administrative Order 2022-015; OMB Circular A-130

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the official brand identity standards, visual guidelines, typography rules, color specifications, logo usage requirements, and template mandates for all communications materials produced by or on behalf of the Federal Consolidated Agency (FCA). Consistent brand identity reinforces the Agency's professionalism, builds public trust, and ensures recognition across all media channels.

## 2. Scope

This directive applies to all print and digital media products produced by the Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC), all materials produced by individual offices and programs, and all materials produced by contractors on behalf of the FCA.

---

## 3. Agency Seal & Logo

### 3.1 The FCA Agency Seal

3.1.1 The FCA Agency Seal is the primary official mark of the Federal Consolidated Agency. Its use is restricted to:
- Official letterhead and correspondence signed by the Agency Director or Deputy Director
- Formal certificates, awards, and commendations
- Congressional testimony materials
- Official publications designated as "Agency-level" by the PAO
- Identification credentials and badges

3.1.2 The Agency Seal may **not** be used on:
- Promotional materials, flyers, or event posters
- Social media graphics
- Presentation slides (except for Director-level briefings)
- Merchandise, apparel, or promotional items
- Any product produced for or distributed to non-government audiences without PAO approval

3.1.3 The Agency Seal must always appear at full fidelity. It may not be:
- Redrawn, recolored, or modified in any way
- Rotated, skewed, or distorted
- Placed on a patterned or photographic background that reduces legibility
- Reproduced at a size smaller than 1 inch in diameter (print) or 100 pixels (digital)
- Cropped, masked, or partially obscured
- Used as a watermark or background element

### 3.2 The FCA Wordmark

3.2.1 The FCA Wordmark ("Federal Consolidated Agency" in the approved typeface and arrangement) is the standard identity mark for most FCA communications.

3.2.2 The wordmark is available in the following configurations:
- **Horizontal:** Logo icon + "Federal Consolidated Agency" on a single line
- **Stacked:** Logo icon above "Federal Consolidated Agency" on two lines
- **Icon Only:** The FCA shield icon without text (authorized for favicons, app icons, and space-constrained applications only)

3.2.3 Minimum clear space around the wordmark must equal the height of the "F" in "Federal" on all sides. No text, graphics, or design elements may intrude into this clear space.

3.2.4 Minimum reproduction sizes:
| Configuration | Print Minimum | Digital Minimum |
|--------------|---------------|----------------|
| Horizontal | 2 inches wide | 200 pixels wide |
| Stacked | 1.5 inches wide | 150 pixels wide |
| Icon Only | 0.5 inches | 48 pixels |

### 3.3 Sub-Organization Logos

3.3.1 Directorates and Offices at the Directorate level may maintain approved sub-organization logos. These logos:
- Must be approved by the Brand Standards Board (BSB)
- Must incorporate the FCA wordmark or icon
- Must use only colors from the approved brand palette
- Must use only fonts from the approved font library
- May not visually compete with or overshadow the FCA wordmark

3.3.2 Organizations **below** the Directorate level (Divisions, Branches, Teams, Sections) are **not authorized** to maintain separate logos per Directive 1200. These organizations use the parent Directorate's logo or the FCA wordmark.

3.3.3 Program and initiative logos (temporary) must include an expiration review date and are valid for a maximum of 3 years before requiring BSB re-approval.

## 4. Color Palette

### 4.1 Primary Brand Colors

The FCA primary brand colors must be used as the dominant colors in all Agency communications:

| Color Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Pantone (PMS) | Usage |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|--------------|-------|
| Agency Blue | #1B3A5C | 27, 58, 92 | 100, 68, 23, 15 | PMS 2758 C | Primary — headers, backgrounds, key elements |
| Agency Gray | #4A5568 | 74, 85, 104 | 58, 42, 26, 15 | PMS 7546 C | Secondary — body text, borders, subtle elements |
| Agency Gold | #D4A843 | 212, 168, 67 | 15, 30, 85, 0 | PMS 7409 C | Accent — highlights, calls-to-action, badges |
| White | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | — | Backgrounds, reversed text |

### 4.2 Secondary Brand Colors

Secondary colors are used sparingly for emphasis, data visualization, callouts, and category differentiation:

| Color Name | Hex | RGB | Pantone (PMS) | Usage |
|-----------|-----|-----|--------------|-------|
| Forest Green | #2D6A4F | 45, 106, 79 | PMS 349 C | Success states, environmental programs |
| Alert Red | #C53030 | 197, 48, 48 | PMS 1797 C | Warnings, errors, urgent notices ONLY |
| Sky Blue | #63B3ED | 99, 179, 237 | PMS 2925 C | Informational callouts, links |
| Warm Gray | #A0AEC0 | 160, 174, 192 | PMS 429 C | Disabled states, dividers, backgrounds |
| Light Gold | #F6E8C1 | 246, 232, 193 | PMS 7401 C | Highlight backgrounds, featured content |
| Dark Navy | #0F2440 | 15, 36, 64 | PMS 2768 C | Footer backgrounds, high-contrast areas |

### 4.3 Color Usage Rules

4.3.1 **Agency Blue** must be the dominant brand color in any product. It should appear in the primary header, navigation, or visual element.

4.3.2 **Alert Red** is reserved exclusively for:
- Error messages and warnings
- Urgent or safety-critical notices
- Required field indicators
- Emergency communications

Alert Red may **not** be used decoratively, as a design accent, or for general emphasis. Any product using Alert Red for non-alert purposes will be rejected during design review.

4.3.3 No more than **four (4)** colors from the brand palette should appear in a single product (excluding photographs and data visualizations).

4.3.4 Colors not in the approved palette may **not** be used unless authorized by the BSB for a specific campaign with a defined expiration date.

4.3.5 Tints (lighter values) of approved colors are permitted for backgrounds and secondary elements. Approved tint ranges:
- 10%, 20%, 40%, 60%, and 80% tints of any primary or secondary color
- Tints must be generated from the exact hex values above, not approximated

## 5. Typography

### 5.1 Approved Font Library

The FCA has standardized on the following font families for all communications:

| Font Family | Classification | Primary Use | License |
|------------|---------------|------------|---------|
| **Inter** | Sans-serif | Primary — headings, body text (digital) | Open source (SIL OFL) |
| **Source Sans Pro** | Sans-serif | Alternate — body text (digital & print) | Open source (SIL OFL) |
| **Merriweather** | Serif | Formal documents, long-form reading | Open source (SIL OFL) |
| **Roboto** | Sans-serif | Data-heavy content, tables, dashboards | Open source (Apache 2.0) |
| **Noto Sans** | Sans-serif | Multilingual content, accessibility | Open source (SIL OFL) |
| **JetBrains Mono** | Monospace | Code samples, technical references | Open source (SIL OFL) |

### 5.2 Font Usage Rules

5.2.1 **Digital products:** Inter is the default primary font. Source Sans Pro is the acceptable alternative.

5.2.2 **Print products:** Inter or Source Sans Pro for headings; Merriweather or Source Sans Pro for body text.

5.2.3 **Formal/legal documents:** Merriweather for body text; Inter for headings.

5.2.4 **Presentations:** Inter for headings and body; Roboto for data tables and charts.

5.2.5 **No product may use more than three (3) font families.** Using more than three requires BSB waiver.

5.2.6 Font weights authorized for use:
- Regular (400) — body text
- Medium (500) — subheadings, emphasis
- Semi-Bold (600) — section headings
- Bold (700) — primary headings, titles

5.2.7 Light (300) and Thin (100) weights are **not authorized** for body text or content in accessibility-sensitive products due to readability concerns. They may be used decoratively in headlines at sizes 24pt/32px or larger.

5.2.8 Italic styles are permitted for:
- Publication titles and references
- Defined terms on first use
- Emphasis (sparingly)

5.2.9 **All caps** text is permitted only for:
- Classification markings and banners
- Button text (limited to 3 words)
- Acronyms and abbreviations

Extended use of all caps for body text, paragraphs, or headings is prohibited per accessibility guidelines.

### 5.3 Type Sizing Standards

| Element | Print (pt) | Digital (px) | Line Height |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Display/Title | 28-36 | 36-48 | 1.1-1.2 |
| Heading 1 | 22-26 | 28-32 | 1.2-1.3 |
| Heading 2 | 18-20 | 22-26 | 1.2-1.3 |
| Heading 3 | 14-16 | 18-22 | 1.3 |
| Body Text | 10-12 | 14-16 | 1.5-1.6 |
| Small/Caption | 8-9 | 12-13 | 1.4 |
| Legal/Footnote | 7-8 | 10-11 | 1.3 |

5.3.1 Body text below 10pt (print) or 14px (digital) is prohibited except for legal disclaimers, footnotes, and source citations.

5.3.2 Minimum contrast ratio for text: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text (18pt/24px or larger), per WCAG 2.1 AA.

## 6. Photography & Imagery Standards

### 6.1 Photography Style

6.1.1 Agency photography should convey:
- Professionalism and competence
- Diversity and inclusion
- Modern, clean aesthetic
- Authentic work environments (avoid overly staged or stock-looking photos)

6.1.2 Preferred photography styles:
- Natural lighting when possible
- Candid or semi-posed (not stiffly posed)
- Environmental context showing the work setting
- Warm, natural color grading (avoid heavy filters or dramatic color shifts)

### 6.2 Stock Photography

6.2.1 Stock photography is acceptable when agency-specific photography is unavailable.

6.2.2 Stock images must come from PDMC-licensed libraries (Adobe Stock or Shutterstock). Images from Unsplash, Pexels, or other free libraries require license verification by the PDMC before use.

6.2.3 Stock images must:
- Look natural and authentic (avoid clichéd "business" photos)
- Represent diverse individuals and settings
- Be relevant to the content (no decorative-only stock photos)
- Not include identifiable brand names, logos, or products

### 6.3 Image Treatment

6.3.1 Photos may be cropped, resized, and color-corrected but may **not** be:
- Materially altered to change the apparent content or context
- Combined with other images to create misleading composites
- Processed with heavy filters that alter the mood beyond the original intent
- Flipped horizontally (text, badges, and insignia would appear reversed)

## 7. Layout & Composition Standards

### 7.1 Grid System

7.1.1 All print and digital products should use a consistent grid system:
- **Print:** 12-column grid with 0.125" gutters on 8.5×11" layouts
- **Digital/Web:** 12-column responsive grid with 16px/24px gutters

7.1.2 Content elements should align to the grid. Designers may break the grid intentionally for creative emphasis, but the overall structure should be grid-based.

### 7.2 Margins & Safe Areas

| Format | Margin (print) | Padding (digital) |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Standard documents | 0.75" all sides | 16-24px |
| Posters | 0.5" minimum | 24-32px |
| Banners | 2" from all edges (safe area) | N/A |
| Business cards | 0.125" bleed, 0.125" safety | N/A |
| Brochures | 0.25" minimum, 0.125" bleed | N/A |

### 7.3 White Space

7.3.1 Products must maintain adequate white space for readability and visual breathing room. As a guideline, at least 30% of the total product area should be white space (backgrounds, margins, spacing between elements).

7.3.2 The PDMC design team has authority to reject requestor-supplied layouts that are excessively dense or cluttered.

## 8. Templates

### 8.1 Mandatory Template Usage

8.1.1 The following product types **must** use PDMC-approved templates:
- Letterhead
- Business cards
- PowerPoint presentations
- Email templates
- Internal memo format
- Organizational charts
- Fact sheets

8.1.2 Templates are maintained in the PDMC Template Library and updated annually (or as policy changes require).

8.1.3 Requestors may not modify template structural elements (header placement, logo position, footer content, margin sizes). Content areas within templates may be customized.

### 8.2 Template Request

8.2.1 Organizations requiring a new template (for a product type not currently covered) must submit a Template Development Request to the BSB.

8.2.2 Template development is a separate project from individual product requests and follows the standard production process plus BSB approval.

## 9. Co-Branding & Partner Logos

### 9.1 When Co-Branding Is Authorized

9.1.1 Co-branding (displaying the FCA logo alongside another organization's logo) is authorized for:
- Inter-agency MOUs and joint programs
- Congressionally mandated initiatives with partner agencies
- Official events co-hosted with partner organizations

9.1.2 Co-branding requires PAO approval in all cases.

### 9.2 Co-Branding Rules

9.2.1 The FCA logo must appear:
- At equal or greater prominence compared to partner logos
- To the left of (or above) partner logos in horizontal (or vertical) arrangements
- With standard clear space maintained regardless of partner logo placement

9.2.2 Partner logos may not be modified, and the partner organization must provide their official logo files.

9.2.3 No more than **four (4)** logos may appear on a single product, including the FCA logo. Products with more than four partner organizations should use a text-based attribution instead.

## 10. Prohibited Visual Elements

### 10.1 The following visual elements are **never permitted** in FCA products:

10.1.1 **Clip art** from Microsoft Office, Google, or free internet sources

10.1.2 **Word art** (decorative text effects from office software)

10.1.3 **Animated GIF decorations** (non-content animations like spinning stars, bouncing text, etc.)

10.1.4 **Drop shadows** on text (subtle shadows on images or cards are acceptable)

10.1.5 **Beveled or embossed text effects**

10.1.6 **Gradient text fill** (gradients on backgrounds and shapes are acceptable)

10.1.7 **Comic Sans, Papyrus, Curlz MT**, or other informal/novelty fonts

10.1.8 **Emojis** in official documents, formal communications, or products bearing the Agency Seal. Emojis are permitted only in social media assets approved by PAO.

10.1.9 **QR codes linking to personal URLs**, URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), or non-.gov/.mil/.edu websites

## 11. Accessibility Standards

### 11.1 All FCA media products must comply with:
- Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (29 U.S.C. § 794d)
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA (for digital products)
- PDF/UA (ISO 14289-1) for PDF documents

### 11.2 Specific Accessibility Requirements:
- (a) All images must have alt-text descriptions
- (b) Color must not be the sole means of conveying information
- (c) Text contrast must meet minimum ratios (see Section 5.3.2)
- (d) Documents must have proper heading hierarchy (H1 > H2 > H3, no skipped levels)
- (e) Tables must have header rows defined
- (f) Links must have descriptive text (no "click here")
- (g) Videos must have closed captions and audio descriptions

---

## 12. Brand Compliance Enforcement

### 12.1 Review Authority

12.1.1 The PDMC design team enforces brand compliance during the production process. Products that do not comply with this directive will be modified to comply before delivery.

12.1.2 The BSB conducts quarterly brand audits of published materials and may require corrections to non-compliant products.

### 12.2 Waivers

12.2.1 Waivers to any provision of this directive may be granted by the BSB for specific, time-limited purposes. Waiver requests must include:
- The specific provision(s) to be waived
- The business justification
- The proposed alternative approach
- The duration of the waiver

12.2.2 Waivers are valid for a maximum of 12 months and must be renewed if still needed.
