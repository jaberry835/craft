# DIRECTIVE 1200 — Information Classification & Security Requirements for Media Products

**Issuing Authority:** Security Directorate, in coordination with the Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC)
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025
**Last Revised:** March 22, 2026
**Supersedes:** DIRECTIVE 1200 (dated June 10, 2023)
**Reference:** Executive Order 13526, 32 CFR Part 2001, FCA Security Manual Chapter 11

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the information security requirements, classification handling procedures, content restrictions, and review processes applicable to all print and digital media products produced by or on behalf of the Federal Consolidated Agency (FCA). Compliance with this directive is mandatory for all PDMC personnel, requestors, reviewers, and approving officials.

## 2. Classification Levels

The FCA recognizes the following classification levels for media product requests:

### 2.1 Unclassified (U)

2.1.1 **Definition:** Information that does not meet the criteria for classification under Executive Order 13526 and is not otherwise controlled.

2.1.2 **Content Restrictions for Unclassified Products:**
- (a) May not contain the names of specific internal divisions, branches, or organizational units below the Directorate level. Use generic references such as "the responsible office" or "the supporting team."
- (b) May not include internal office symbols, routing codes, or organizational identifiers.
- (c) May not reference specific building numbers, room numbers, or facility locations beyond publicly available campus-level addresses.
- (d) May not include personal mobile phone numbers, personal email addresses, or home addresses of any personnel.
- (e) May include publicly available contact information such as main office phone numbers and official ".gov" email addresses.
- (f) May reference the agency name ("Federal Consolidated Agency" or "FCA") and Directorate-level names only.

2.1.3 **Description Requirements:** Requestors must affirm in the description field that the product contains no classified, FOUO, or CUI information. Include the statement: "This product contains only Unclassified information approved for public release" if the product will be externally distributed.

2.1.4 **Handling:** No special handling requirements. Standard PDMC production workflow applies.

### 2.2 For Official Use Only (FOUO)

2.2.1 **Definition:** Unclassified information that is exempt from public release under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and requires safeguarding from unauthorized disclosure.

2.2.2 **Content Allowances for FOUO Products:**
- (a) May reference internal organizational units down to the Division level (but not Branch or below).
- (b) May include internal telephone directories and ".gov" email addresses.
- (c) May reference building numbers and general campus locations (but not specific room numbers of sensitive spaces).
- (d) May include budget figures, staffing numbers, and operational statistics that are pre-decisional or deliberative.
- (e) May include names and titles of personnel at the GS-13/equivalent level and above.
- (f) May **not** include names or identifying information of personnel below GS-13 without their written consent (Form FCA-812).
- (g) May **not** include investigative findings, legal opinions, or personnel actions.

2.2.3 **Marking Requirements:**
- All pages of print products must bear the marking "FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY" centered at the top and bottom of each page.
- Digital products must include a "FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY" banner or watermark.
- The PDMC will apply standard FOUO markings during production. The requestor is responsible for confirming marking accuracy during the review cycle.

2.2.4 **Distribution Restrictions:**
- FOUO products may only be distributed to government employees and contractors with a need-to-know.
- FOUO products may **not** be posted on publicly accessible websites, shared via personal email, or distributed outside government networks.
- Electronic copies must be transmitted via government email or secure file transfer only.

2.2.5 **Production Environment:** FOUO products are produced on the PDMC standard production network. No special workstation is required.

### 2.3 Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)

2.3.1 **Definition:** Information the government creates or possesses, or that an entity creates or possesses for or on behalf of the government, that a law, regulation, or government-wide policy requires or permits the agency to handle using safeguarding or dissemination controls, as defined by 32 CFR Part 2002.

2.3.2 **Content Allowances for CUI Products:**
- (a) All FOUO allowances apply.
- (b) May include organizational references down to the Branch level.
- (c) May include facility-specific information such as building numbers, floor numbers, and wing identifiers.
- (d) May include Privacy Act-protected information (PII) **only** when operationally necessary and approved by the Privacy Officer.
- (e) May include law enforcement sensitive (LES) information when properly marked.
- (f) May include proprietary business information under appropriate CUI categories.
- (g) May **not** include intelligence sources and methods.
- (h) May **not** include Special Access Program (SAP) information.

2.3.3 **Marking Requirements:**
- All pages must bear "CUI" or the specific CUI category banner (e.g., "CUI//SP-PRVCY" for privacy data) centered at the top and bottom.
- The CUI designation indicator must appear on the first page: "Controlled by: [Office]" and "CUI Category: [Category]" and "Dissemination: [Limited/Standard]."
- Digital products must include embedded CUI metadata tags in file properties.

2.3.4 **Production Environment:**
- CUI products must be produced on PDMC workstations connected to the agency's CUI-accredited network segment.
- Production files must be stored on encrypted, access-controlled file shares.
- CUI products may not be produced on personal devices, unaccredited systems, or cloud services not authorized for CUI.

2.3.5 **Approval Requirements:**
- All CUI requests require the requestor's Information Owner to confirm the CUI designation and category before production begins.
- Requests involving CUI must include the "CUI Designation Certification" (Form FCA-830) as an attachment or reference number in the description.

### 2.4 Super Classified (SC)

2.4.1 **Definition:** A special FCA-internal classification category reserved for information pertaining to agency strategic initiatives, executive leadership communications, pre-decisional policy of agency-wide impact, and other materials designated by the Agency Director or Deputy Director. This classification is unique to FCA internal governance and does not correspond to national security classification levels.

> **IMPORTANT:** Super Classified is not equivalent to Confidential, Secret, or Top Secret under E.O. 13526. Products containing national security classified information are **not** processed through the PDMC and must be coordinated with the Classified Production Facility (CPF).

2.4.2 **Content Allowances for Super Classified Products:**
- (a) All CUI allowances apply.
- (b) May include information about planned organizational restructuring, RIFs, or position realignments.
- (c) May include pre-decisional budget allocations, resource distribution plans, and strategic investment proposals.
- (d) May include executive leadership internal communications summaries.
- (e) May include results of internal studies, organizational assessments, and climate surveys before public release.
- (f) May include acquisition-sensitive information prior to award.

2.4.3 **Content Restrictions:**
- (a) May **not** include national security classified information (Confidential, Secret, Top Secret).
- (b) May **not** include intelligence information or information derived from intelligence sources.
- (c) May **not** include information subject to statutory classification requirements under authorities other than the FCA Director.

2.4.4 **Marking Requirements:**
- All pages must bear "SUPER CLASSIFIED — FCA INTERNAL" in red text, centered at the top and bottom of each page.
- Cover pages must include: "SUPER CLASSIFIED — FCA INTERNAL — DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTED — NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE"
- A numbered copy control sheet must accompany all Super Classified print products.
- Digital files must be password-protected and encrypted using AES-256 encryption.

2.4.5 **Production Environment:**
- Super Classified products must be produced in the PDMC Secure Production Room (SPR), Building 1, Room B-104.
- Production is limited to PDMC personnel with Super Classified access authorization (Form FCA-SC-100, approved by the Security Director).
- No more than two (2) PDMC personnel may work on a Super Classified product at any time.
- All production files, drafts, and work-in-progress materials must be stored on the isolated Super Classified server (SC-SRV-01). No copies on standard network drives.

2.4.6 **Approval Requirements:**
- **All** Super Classified requests require Security Director approval before production begins. This is in addition to the standard financial approval chain per Directive 1050.
- The requestor must be at the GS-15 or Senior Executive Service (SES) level, or hold an explicit delegation from an SES member.
- Super Classified requests must include the SC Authorization Number assigned by the Security Director.

2.4.7 **Distribution:**
- Print copies are numbered and tracked via the Controlled Distribution Log (FCA-SC-200).
- Digital copies may only be transmitted via the SC-accredited secure communication system.
- Recipients must be individually approved by the originator.
- No mass distribution of Super Classified products is authorized.

## 3. Classification Determination Responsibilities

### 3.1 Requestor Responsibilities

3.1.1 The requestor is the **Original Classification Authority (OCA)** for purposes of media production requests and bears full responsibility for:
- Accurately determining the classification level of the content
- Ensuring all content conforms to the restrictions for the designated classification level
- Providing complete and accurate descriptions that reflect the nature and sensitivity of the content
- Completing all required security forms and obtaining necessary approvals before submission

3.1.2 If the requestor is uncertain about the proper classification level, they **must** consult with the Security Directorate's Classification Management Officer (CMO) before submitting the request. The PDMC will not adjudicate classification disputes.

### 3.2 PDMC Responsibilities

3.2.1 The PDMC will:
- Verify that required security forms and approval numbers are present before beginning production
- Apply correct security markings to all products consistent with the designated classification level
- Ensure products are produced in the appropriate production environment
- Report any suspected security violations or classification discrepancies to the Security Directorate

3.2.2 The PDMC **does not**:
- Make classification determinations
- Waive any security requirements specified in this directive
- Accept verbal classifications — all classification determinations must be documented

## 4. Content Review & Pre-Production Security Screening

### 4.1 Automated Screening

4.1.1 All request descriptions are subject to automated keyword screening by the PDMC intake system. The following categories of content trigger an automatic security flag:
- Names matching the FCA Sensitive Personnel List
- References to specific security systems, access controls, or surveillance equipment
- Building numbers associated with restricted or sensitive facilities (Buildings 7, 12, 15, 21, and Annex C)
- Program names on the Protected Programs List
- References to ongoing investigations, audits, or legal proceedings

4.1.2 Flagged requests are placed in a security hold queue and require manual review by the PDMC Security Liaison before proceeding.

### 4.2 Manual Security Review

4.2.1 The following request types **always** require manual security review regardless of content:
- All Super Classified requests
- All requests from the Security Directorate, Inspector General, or Office of General Counsel
- Requests referencing inter-agency agreements or foreign government coordination
- Requests involving photographs or imagery of agency facilities
- Requests for products to be distributed at external events, conferences, or to media outlets

4.2.2 The manual security review process adds 2-5 business days to the standard production timeline. This is non-negotiable and cannot be expedited.

## 5. Security Violations

### 5.1 Improper Classification

5.1.1 Submitting a request with a classification level lower than warranted by the content (underclassification) constitutes a security incident and must be reported via FCA Incident Report (FCA-IR-001).

5.1.2 First-time underclassification incidents result in mandatory security refresher training for the requestor. Repeated incidents may result in suspension of PDMC access and referral to the Security Directorate for administrative action.

### 5.2 Unauthorized Disclosure

5.2.1 If FOUO, CUI, or Super Classified product content is found on unauthorized systems, networks, or in the possession of unauthorized individuals:
- The PDMC will immediately cease production and quarantine all related files
- The Security Directorate will be notified within 1 hour of discovery
- The requestor and their supervisory chain will be notified within 4 hours
- A formal investigation will be initiated per FCA Security Manual, Chapter 14

---

## 6. Cross-Classification Products

### 6.1 Mixed Content

6.1.1 When a single product contains content at multiple classification levels, the **entire product** must be marked and handled at the highest classification level contained within.

6.1.2 Requestors are encouraged to separate products into classification-appropriate segments where feasible to minimize handling restrictions.

### 6.2 Derivative Products

6.2.1 Products derived from existing classified materials must carry the same or higher classification as the source material. Downgrading classification requires written approval from the Original Classification Authority of the source material.

---

## 7. Records Management

7.1 Production records for classified products (FOUO, CUI, Super Classified) must be retained for a minimum of 6 years per Directive 1000, Section 8.

7.2 Super Classified production audit logs are maintained indefinitely or until disposition is directed by the Agency Records Officer.

7.3 Destruction of classified media production materials (proofs, drafts, plates, test prints) must be accomplished using agency-approved destruction methods:
- Paper: Cross-cut shredding (DIN Level P-4 or higher)
- Digital: Secure deletion per NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1
