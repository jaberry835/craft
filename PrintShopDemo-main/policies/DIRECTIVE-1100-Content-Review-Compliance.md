# DIRECTIVE 1100 — Content Review, Compliance & Description Standards

**Issuing Authority:** Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC), in coordination with the Public Affairs Office (PAO) and Office of General Counsel (OGC)
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Revised:** May 5, 2026
**Reference:** Directive 1000, Section 6; Directive 1200; 5 CFR Part 2635

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the content review processes, compliance requirements, prohibited content categories, mandatory disclaimers, description field standards, and quality assurance protocols for all print and digital media products produced by the Print & Digital Media Center. Proper content compliance ensures that all FCA media products conform to federal regulations, agency policies, and professional standards.

## 2. Request Description Standards

### 2.1 Minimum Description Requirements

2.1.1 All request descriptions must contain a **minimum of 50 characters** (per Directive 1000, Section 3.2). Descriptions below this threshold will be automatically rejected.

2.1.2 The description must provide sufficient detail for PDMC designers to produce the product without requiring additional clarification. Vague descriptions (e.g., "make a poster," "need a graphic," "design something nice") will be returned for elaboration.

2.1.3 The description must be written in English. Multilingual products must include the English content as the primary description with translations clearly noted.

### 2.2 Required Description Elements by Service Type

#### 2.2.1 Print — Posters

The description **must** include:
- (a) Poster purpose and display context (office hallway, lobby, conference, event, outdoor)
- (b) Complete text content — all headlines, body copy, dates, locations, contact information
- (c) Imagery guidance — photos to include (attach as files), illustration style, or icons desired
- (d) Color scheme preference or reference to a campaign/brand
- (e) Desired orientation (portrait or landscape)
- (f) Target audience (internal employees, visitors, public)
- (g) Display duration — permanent or temporary (with takedown date)

#### 2.2.2 Print — Banners

The description **must** include:
- (a) Banner type (vinyl, fabric, retractable stand)
- (b) Installation location and method (grommets, pole pockets, stand, wall-mounted)
- (c) Indoor or outdoor use (outdoor requires UV-resistant materials)
- (d) Complete text content including any sponsor logos or co-branding
- (e) Viewing distance estimate (affects font size requirements — minimum 1 inch of letter height per 10 feet of viewing distance)
- (f) Event date (if for a specific event)

#### 2.2.3 Print — Copies & Reproductions

The description **must** include:
- (a) Source document identification (title, author, date, version)
- (b) Whether the source is print-ready or requires formatting
- (c) Paper type preference (standard 20lb, premium 28lb, card stock, specialty)
- (d) Binding or finishing requirements (stapled, hole-punched, spiral-bound, perfect-bound)
- (e) Collation requirements (if multiple documents are to be assembled into sets)
- (f) Distribution list or pickup instructions

#### 2.2.4 Print — Business Cards

The description **must** include:
- (a) Full name exactly as it should appear on the card
- (b) Official title/position
- (c) Organization name (Directorate level only per Directive 1200, Section 2.1)
- (d) Office phone number (must be an official agency number)
- (e) Official email address (must end in ".gov")
- (f) Office location (building and room number — **Unclassified cards may not include room numbers per Directive 1200**)
- (g) Any approved certifications or professional designations to display

**Prohibited on business cards:**
- Personal mobile numbers
- Personal email addresses
- Social media handles (unless officially designated PAO accounts)
- Organization names below the Division level (per Directive 1200)
- Military or honorary titles not associated with current position

#### 2.2.5 Print — Brochures

The description **must** include:
- (a) Fold type (bi-fold, tri-fold, gate-fold, z-fold)
- (b) Complete panel-by-panel content (specify which text goes on which panel)
- (c) Cover panel design direction
- (d) Back panel content (often includes contact information or QR code)
- (e) Call-to-action or desired reader response
- (f) Distribution method (handout, mailer, display rack)

#### 2.2.6 Print — Letterhead & Envelopes

The description **must** include:
- (a) Organization name and address exactly as it should appear
- (b) Logo placement preference (pre-positioned per Directive 925 standards)
- (c) Whether a matching envelope design is needed
- (d) Paper stock preference (standard white, cotton bond, linen)
- (e) Whether this is a new design or reorder of existing design (include previous order number)

#### 2.2.7 Print — Large Format

The description **must** include:
- (a) Exact dimensions (width × height in inches)
- (b) Print-ready file specifications or design requirements
- (c) Mounting requirements (none, foam board, gator board, aluminum composite)
- (d) Lamination requirements (none, gloss, matte, anti-glare)
- (e) Display method (easel, wall-mounted, hanging, free-standing)
- (f) Whether this will be part of a multi-panel display (provide layout diagram)

#### 2.2.8 Print — Booklets

The description **must** include:
- (a) Page count (including cover; must be a multiple of 4 for saddle-stitch)
- (b) Complete content for all pages (or reference to attached manuscript)
- (c) Binding method preference (saddle-stitch, perfect-bound, spiral, wire-o)
- (d) Cover stock preference (glossy, matte, laminated)
- (e) Table of contents requirements
- (f) ISBN or publication number (if applicable — required for publications over 48 pages)

#### 2.2.9 Digital — Graphics & Illustrations

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 3.2.

#### 2.2.10 Digital — Logo Design

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 4.3, including the BSB Pre-Approval Reference Number.

#### 2.2.11 Digital — Social Media Assets

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 5.2.

#### 2.2.12 Digital — Web Banners & Headers

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 6.3.

#### 2.2.13 Digital — Email Templates

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 7.2.

#### 2.2.14 Digital — Presentations

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 8.2.

#### 2.2.15 Digital — Infographics

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 9.2.

#### 2.2.16 Digital — Video & Motion Graphics

The description **must** include all elements specified in Directive 1020, Section 10.2.

## 3. Prohibited Content

### 3.1 Universally Prohibited Content

The following content is prohibited in **all** PDMC products regardless of classification level, media type, or intended audience:

3.1.1 **Political Content:**
- (a) Names, images, slogans, or logos of political parties, candidates, or political action committees
- (b) Endorsements of or opposition to any political candidate, party, or ballot measure
- (c) Content that could reasonably be perceived as using government resources for political purposes per the Hatch Act (5 U.S.C. §§ 7321-7326)
- (d) Campaign-related imagery, colors, or phraseology associated with active political campaigns

3.1.2 **Commercial Endorsements:**
- (a) Brand names, logos, or trademarks of commercial entities (except when referencing government contracts or programs by their official name)
- (b) Statements that appear to endorse, promote, or favor any commercial product, service, or company
- (c) QR codes or URLs linking to commercial websites (government and .mil/.gov/.edu sites only)

3.1.3 **Discriminatory Content:**
- Content that discriminates on the basis of race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, sexual orientation, and gender identity), national origin, age, disability, or genetic information, per Title VII and other applicable federal anti-discrimination statutes

3.1.4 **Personally Identifiable Information (PII) without Authorization:**
- Social Security Numbers (never permitted in any media product)
- Financial account numbers
- Medical information
- Home addresses, personal phone numbers, or personal email addresses
- Date of birth (except in official biographical materials approved by the individual)

3.1.5 **Copyrighted Material:**
- (a) Images, text, music, or other creative works copied from the internet, publications, or other sources without proper licensing or government use rights
- (b) "Royalty-free" images from non-approved stock libraries (the PDMC maintains approved library subscriptions)
- (c) AI-generated content used without disclosure and PAO approval

### 3.2 Classification-Specific Prohibitions

In addition to the universal prohibitions above:

**Unclassified Products may not contain:**
- Internal organization names below the Directorate level (per Directive 1200, Section 2.1.2)
- Internal office symbols or routing codes
- Specific building/room numbers beyond publicly available addresses
- References to internal-only programs, projects, or systems by their actual names
- Names of individuals below the GS-15/SES level (unless public-facing spokesperson roles)

**FOUO Products may not contain:**
- Names of individuals below GS-13 without written consent
- Investigative findings or legal opinions
- Personnel action details (promotions, disciplinary actions, terminations)

**CUI Products may not contain:**
- Intelligence sources or methods
- Special Access Program (SAP) information
- Information subject to ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) without State Department approval

**Super Classified Products may not contain:**
- National security classified information (Confidential, Secret, Top Secret)
- Intelligence-derived information
- Information classified under authorities other than the FCA Director

## 4. Mandatory Disclaimers & Legal Text

### 4.1 External Distribution Disclaimer

All products intended for distribution outside the Federal Consolidated Agency must include:
```
"This product is produced by the Federal Consolidated Agency (FCA). 
The contents do not necessarily represent the official views or policies 
of the United States Government."
```

### 4.2 Event Materials Disclaimer

Products promoting or supporting agency-sponsored events must include:
```
"The Federal Consolidated Agency does not endorse any commercial 
product, service, or company mentioned or depicted in this material."
```

### 4.3 Accessibility Statement

Products published on agency websites must include or link to:
```
"The Federal Consolidated Agency is committed to making its electronic 
and information technologies accessible to individuals with disabilities 
in accordance with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (29 U.S.C. § 794d)."
```

### 4.4 Environmental Statement

Print products exceeding 500 units must include:
```
"Printed on paper containing a minimum of 30% post-consumer recycled content 
in accordance with Executive Order 13693."
```

### 4.5 Classification Markings

All classification markings must appear as specified in Directive 1200. The PDMC applies markings during production; the requestor is responsible for verifying accuracy during the review cycle.

## 5. Content Review Process

### 5.1 PDMC Design Review

5.1.1 All products undergo an internal PDMC design review before the first proof is shared with the requestor. This review covers:
- Brand compliance (colors, fonts, logo usage per Directive 925)
- Layout quality and typographic standards
- Image resolution and quality
- Spelling and grammar (of text provided by requestor — PDMC does not edit for factual accuracy)
- Required disclaimers and markings
- Accessibility compliance (Section 508 for digital products)

### 5.2 Requestor Review

5.2.1 The requestor receives up to three (3) proofs for review per production cycle. Each proof cycle allows for corrections and modifications.

5.2.2 The requestor must respond to each proof within **5 business days**. Proofs not responded to within 5 business days trigger an automatic reminder. Proofs not responded to within **10 business days** result in the request being placed on Administrative Hold (per Directive 1000, Section 7).

5.2.3 The requestor's final proof approval constitutes acceptance of the product's content, accuracy, and compliance. The PDMC is not liable for content errors in products that received requestor final approval.

### 5.3 Public Affairs Office (PAO) Review

5.3.1 The following product types **require** PAO review before final production:
- All products intended for external distribution (public, media, inter-agency partners)
- Social media assets (per Directive 1020, Section 5.3)
- Press releases, media advisories, and fact sheets
- Products featuring photographs of agency facilities or operations
- Products promoting agency events open to the public
- Products with agency spokesperson quotes or attributable statements
- Video and motion graphic products for public release

5.3.2 PAO review adds **3-5 business days** to the production timeline. The PDMC will submit products to PAO on behalf of the requestor.

5.3.3 PAO may request modifications, add disclaimers, or reject products that do not meet public communications standards.

### 5.4 Legal Review

5.4.1 Products are referred to the Office of General Counsel (OGC) when they contain:
- Legal citations or regulatory references
- Rights and responsibilities statements
- Terms and conditions
- Copyright or intellectual property claims
- Content that could create agency liability

5.4.2 OGC review adds **5-10 business days** to the production timeline.

## 6. AI-Generated Content Policy

### 6.1 Disclosure Requirements

6.1.1 Any request that incorporates AI-generated text, imagery, or design elements must disclose this in the description field with the statement: "This request includes AI-generated content."

6.1.2 The requestor must identify which specific elements are AI-generated.

### 6.2 Review Requirements

6.2.1 AI-generated content is subject to enhanced review for:
- Factual accuracy (AI hallucinations and fabricated data)
- Copyright compliance (AI models may reproduce copyrighted content)
- Bias and appropriateness
- Brand consistency

6.2.2 AI-generated images must be reviewed and approved by the PAO before use in any PDMC product.

### 6.3 Restrictions

6.3.1 AI-generated content may not be used in:
- Products bearing the official Agency Seal
- Legal documents, regulatory notices, or formal correspondence
- Products classified as CUI or Super Classified
- Official biographical materials

## 7. Quality Assurance Standards

### 7.1 Typography Standards

7.1.1 Minimum body text size: 10pt (print), 14px (digital)
7.1.2 Minimum heading text size: 14pt (print), 18px (digital)
7.1.3 Maximum recommended body text line length: 65-75 characters per line
7.1.4 Text must maintain sufficient contrast against its background (minimum WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text)
7.1.5 No more than three (3) font families per product

### 7.2 Image Quality Standards

7.2.1 Raster images for print: minimum 300 DPI at final reproduction size
7.2.2 Raster images for web: minimum 72 DPI (150 DPI preferred for retina displays)
7.2.3 Images must not appear pixelated, stretched, or distorted at final reproduction size
7.2.4 All images must be properly color-profiled for the output medium

### 7.3 Proofreading

7.3.1 The PDMC performs a single round of proofreading for spelling and grammar. However, the requestor bears ultimate responsibility for content accuracy.

7.3.2 The PDMC does not verify:
- Factual claims, statistics, or data accuracy
- Proper names, titles, or credentials
- Date and time accuracy
- Phone numbers, email addresses, or URLs
- Regulatory citations or legal accuracy

## 8. Intellectual Property & Usage Rights

### 8.1 Government Works

8.1.1 All media products produced by PDMC personnel (federal employees) are works of the United States Government and are not subject to copyright protection per 17 U.S.C. § 105.

### 8.2 Contractor-Produced Works

8.2.1 Media products produced by PDMC contractor personnel may be subject to different intellectual property terms depending on the contract. The PDMC maintains usage rights documentation for all contractor-produced works.

### 8.3 Third-Party Content

8.3.1 Requestors must not include copyrighted content from third parties without providing proof of licensing or permission.

8.3.2 The PDMC maintains enterprise licenses for:
- Adobe Stock (standard and premium images)
- Shutterstock (images and limited video)
- Epidemic Sound (royalty-free music for video productions)

8.3.3 Content from non-licensed sources will be rejected.
