# DIRECTIVE 1050 — Financial Management, Cost Estimation & Approval Authority for Media Products

**Issuing Authority:** Office of the Chief Financial Officer (OCFO), in coordination with the Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC)
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2024
**Last Revised:** April 8, 2026
**Reference:** Directive 1000, Section 4; FCA Financial Management Manual, Chapter 7

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the financial policies, cost estimation methodologies, approval authority thresholds, chargeback procedures, and budgetary controls governing all print and digital media production services provided by the PDMC. Compliance is mandatory for all requesting organizations and approving officials within the Federal Consolidated Agency.

## 2. Cost Estimation Methodology

### 2.1 Standard Cost Schedule

The PDMC maintains a Standard Cost Schedule (SCS) that is updated annually on October 1 (beginning of fiscal year). All cost estimates are generated from the SCS and based on the following factors:

| Cost Factor | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Base Production Cost | Fixed fee per product type (covers design labor, production setup) |
| Material Cost | Variable cost for paper stock, ink, finishing, binding |
| Quantity Multiplier | Per-unit cost applied to quantities beyond the first unit |
| Complexity Surcharge | Applied to products requiring illustration, custom photography, or data visualization |
| Priority Surcharge | Percentage increase for Expedited or Rush priority |
| Security Surcharge | Applied to CUI and Super Classified products for secure handling |
| Revision Surcharge | Applied after the third revision cycle per product |

## 3. Surcharges

### 3.1 Priority Surcharges

| Priority Level | Surcharge | Production Timeline |
|---------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Standard | None (0%) | 10-15 business days |
| Expedited | +25% of total cost | 5-7 business days |
| Rush | +50% of total cost | 1-3 business days |

3.1.1 Priority surcharges are applied to the **total estimated cost** (base + materials + quantity).

3.1.2 Rush priority is available only for products with a total estimated cost under $5,000. Products exceeding $5,000 may not be rushed.

3.1.3 Rush requests submitted after 12:00 PM ET will begin production the following business day.

### 3.2 Security Surcharges

| Classification Level | Surcharge |
|---------------------|-----------|
| Unclassified | None |
| FOUO | +5% (marking and handling) |
| CUI | +15% (secure production, marking, handling) |
| Super Classified | +35% (SPR production, restricted access, handling, secure delivery) |

### 3.3 Revision Surcharges

3.3.1 Each request includes up to **three (3) revision cycles** at no additional charge.

3.3.2 Beginning with the fourth revision cycle, a surcharge of **$75 per revision cycle** is applied (per Directive 1000, Section 4.4).

3.3.3 A "revision cycle" constitutes one round of feedback from the requestor and the resulting modifications by PDMC. Submitting partial feedback across multiple messages constitutes multiple revision cycles.

### 3.4 Overtime / After-Hours Production

3.4.1 Standard PDMC production hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM ET, excluding federal holidays.

3.4.2 Production outside standard hours requires Deputy Director-level approval and incurs a **100% surcharge** on labor costs.

## 4. Approval Authority Thresholds

### 4.1 Standard Approval Chain

The following approval thresholds apply to all media production requests based on the total estimated cost (including all surcharges):

| Estimated Total Cost | Required Approver | Delegation Allowed |
|---------------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| $0 – $500 | Immediate Supervisor (GS-13+ or equivalent) | No |
| $501 – $2,500 | Division Chief (GS-14+ or equivalent) | No |
| $2,501 – $10,000 | Deputy Director (GS-15/SES) | To Division Chief with written delegation |
| $10,001 – $50,000 | Director (SES) | To Deputy Director with written delegation |
| Over $50,000 | Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) AND Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | No |

4.1.1 Approval authority is determined by the **estimated total cost at the time of submission**, not the final cost. If the final cost exceeds the next threshold, additional approval must be obtained before delivery.

4.1.2 Approving officials must have budget authority over the fund cite specified in the request. The PDMC will verify fund cite validity before production.

### 4.2 Priority-Adjusted Approval

4.2.1 **Expedited Priority:** Requires approval from one level above the cost-threshold approver.
- Example: A $400 request at Expedited priority requires Division Chief approval (one above Immediate Supervisor).

4.2.2 **Rush Priority:** Requires approval from two levels above the cost-threshold approver, or at minimum, the Deputy Director level.
- Example: A $200 request at Rush priority requires Deputy Director approval.

4.2.3 These elevated approval requirements ensure that accelerated production is justified and that the associated surcharges are authorized.

### 4.3 Super Classified Approval

4.3.1 All Super Classified requests require **both** the financial approval (per Section 4.1) **and** Security Director approval, regardless of cost.

4.3.2 The Security Director may impose additional conditions on Super Classified requests, including restricting the number of copies, requiring escort for delivery, or mandating destruction timelines.

### 4.4 Blanket Purchase Approvals

4.4.1 Organizations with recurring, predictable media production needs may request a **Blanket Purchase Approval (BPA)** for a fiscal year quarter.

4.4.2 BPA requirements:
- Minimum aggregate value of $5,000 per quarter
- Maximum BPA value of $50,000 per quarter
- Individual requests under the BPA may not exceed $2,500 each
- Approved by the Division Chief and the OCFO Budget Analyst

4.4.3 BPA holders may submit requests that reference their BPA number in lieu of obtaining individual approvals, provided each request is within the per-request limit.

## 5. Fund Cite & Budget Controls

### 5.1 Fund Cite Requirements

5.1.1 Every request must include a valid fund cite in the format:
```
[Agency Code]-[Appropriation]-[Budget Activity]-[Org Code]-[Object Class]-[Project Code]
```
Example: `FCA-0100-BA3-4200-2531-PM2025`

5.1.2 Requests without a valid fund cite will be returned without processing.

5.1.3 The PDMC will validate fund cite availability through the Financial Management Information System (FMIS) before committing to production. Requests with insufficient funds will be placed on hold.

### 5.2 Cost Center Requirements

5.2.1 In addition to the fund cite, each request must specify a cost center to enable organizational chargeback tracking.

5.2.2 The cost center must be a valid six-digit code from the agency Cost Center Directory, updated quarterly by the OCFO.

### 5.3 Cross-Organizational Requests

5.3.1 When one organization requests media production services on behalf of another:
- The requesting organization's fund cite is used unless a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) specifies otherwise.
- Both organizations' Division Chiefs (or above) must sign the request when the estimated cost exceeds $1,000.

### 5.4 Fiscal Year Transition

5.4.1 Requests submitted within the last 15 business days of the fiscal year (approximately September 10-30) that cannot be completed before September 30 must use the following fiscal year's funds.

5.4.2 The PDMC will issue a "Fiscal Year Cutoff Advisory" each August specifying the exact cutoff date and any expedited processing options for end-of-year requests.

## 6. Chargeback and Invoicing

### 6.1 Chargeback Process

6.1.1 The PDMC operates on a full cost-recovery basis. All production costs are charged back to the requesting organization's fund cite.

6.1.2 Chargebacks are processed on the 15th and last business day of each month.

6.1.3 Each chargeback transaction includes: Request ID, product description, quantity, base cost, surcharges, and total cost.

### 6.2 Cost Disputes

6.2.1 Organizations may dispute chargeback amounts within **30 calendar days** of the chargeback posting date.

6.2.2 Disputes must be submitted in writing to the PDMC Program Manager with supporting documentation.

6.2.3 Unresolved disputes are escalated to the OCFO Budget Division for adjudication.

### 6.3 Cancellation Costs

6.3.1 Requests cancelled before production begins: No charge.

6.3.2 Requests cancelled after production begins but before first proof: 25% of estimated cost.

6.3.3 Requests cancelled after first proof delivery: 50% of estimated cost.

6.3.4 Requests cancelled after final proof approval: 100% of estimated cost (product will be completed and delivered).

---

## 7. Annual Budget Planning

7.1 Organizations should include media production costs in their annual operating budget submissions.

7.2 The PDMC publishes a "Media Production Rate Card" by August 1 each year, projecting the following fiscal year's Standard Cost Schedule, to assist organizations with budget planning.

7.3 Historical spending reports are available from the PDMC Finance Team on request.
