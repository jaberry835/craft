# DIRECTIVE 1000 — General Print & Digital Media Services Policy

**Issuing Authority:** Office of the Director, Administrative Services Division (ASD)
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2024
**Last Revised:** January 15, 2026
**Supersedes:** Directive 1000, dated March 12, 2021
**Distribution:** All organizational elements, tenants, and supported activities

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the overarching policies, responsibilities, and procedures governing the request, production, distribution, and archival of all print and digital media products produced by or on behalf of the Federal Consolidated Agency (FCA). It ensures that all media products comply with applicable federal regulations, agency branding standards, classification requirements, fiscal accountability mandates, and operational security guidelines.

## 2. Applicability

This policy applies to:

- All FCA directorates, divisions, branches, and subordinate offices
- All tenant organizations operating under FCA administrative support agreements
- Contractor personnel performing work on behalf of FCA under applicable task orders
- Inter-agency requests processed through FCA's Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC)

## 3. References

- FCA Directive 800 — Records Management and Information Governance
- FCA Directive 925 — Agency Visual Identity and Branding Standards
- FCA Directive 1050 — Financial Management and Obligation Authority
- FCA Directive 1200 — Information Security and Classification Management
- Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), Subpart 8.8 — Acquisition of Printing
- 44 U.S.C. § 501 — Government Publishing Office Requirements
- OMB Circular A-130 — Managing Information as a Strategic Resource

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **PDMC** | Print & Digital Media Center — the agency facility responsible for production of all authorized media products |
| **Requestor** | The individual initiating a media services request on behalf of their organizational element |
| **Approving Official (AO)** | The requestor's immediate supervisor or designated authority with signature authority for media expenditures |
| **Fund Cite** | The specific appropriation and budget line item against which production costs will be charged |
| **Cost Center** | The organizational responsibility center code used for internal cost accounting |
| **Production Order** | The formal work order generated upon approval of a media services request |
| **Digital Asset** | Any electronic media product including but not limited to graphics, logos, templates, presentations, and motion media |
| **Print Asset** | Any physical media product produced through offset, digital, or large-format printing processes |

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 General Requirements

5.1.1 All requests for print and digital media services **shall** be submitted through the PixelPress Request Management System (PRMS). Requests submitted via email, telephone, or walk-in are not authorized and will not be processed.

5.1.2 Every request **must** include a valid fund cite and cost center. Requests without valid funding documentation will be returned without action.

5.1.3 Requestors **must** be government civilian employees (GS/GM/SES) or active-duty military personnel. Contractor personnel may submit requests only when accompanied by a government sponsor endorsement (Form FCA-442).

5.1.4 All media products **must** comply with FCA Directive 925 (Branding Standards). Products that do not conform to approved brand guidelines will be returned for revision.

5.1.5 The PDMC reserves the right to reject, modify, or delay any request that does not comply with applicable policies, funding requirements, or production capabilities.

### 5.2 Request Submission Standards

5.2.1 All requests **must** include the following minimum information:
  - (a) Requestor's full legal name, official email address, and DSN or commercial telephone number
  - (b) Department or directorate name and office symbol
  - (c) Building and room number for the requestor's duty location
  - (d) Approving Official's name and email address
  - (e) Classification level of the requested product
  - (f) A detailed description of the requested product (minimum 50 characters)
  - (g) Valid fund cite and cost center
  - (h) Requested completion date
  - (i) Delivery method preference

5.2.2 Descriptions **must** include sufficient detail to enable production without additional consultation. At minimum, descriptions shall address:
  - The intended purpose and audience for the product
  - Any specific text, imagery, or content to be included
  - Reference to any existing products to be replicated or modified
  - Any applicable regulatory or compliance requirements

5.2.3 Requests lacking required information will be placed in "Pending Clarification" status. Requests remaining in this status for more than 10 business days will be automatically cancelled.

### 5.3 Approval Authority

5.3.1 The following approval thresholds apply to all media services requests:

| Estimated Cost | Required Approval |
|---------------|-------------------|
| $0 – $500 | Requestor's immediate supervisor (GS-13 or above) |
| $501 – $2,500 | Division Chief or equivalent (GS-14 or above) |
| $2,501 – $10,000 | Directorate Deputy Director or equivalent (GS-15 or above) |
| $10,001 – $50,000 | Directorate Director or equivalent (SES/Flag Officer) |
| Over $50,000 | Agency Chief Administrative Officer with CFO concurrence |

5.3.2 Rush requests (1–3 business day turnaround) carry a 50% surcharge and **require** approval one tier above the standard threshold regardless of cost.

5.3.3 Requests involving Super Classified materials **require** approval from the Director of Security (DS) in addition to the standard approval chain, regardless of cost.

### 5.4 Fiscal Responsibility

5.4.1 All costs for media services are charged to the requesting organization's fund cite. The PDMC does not maintain discretionary production funds.

5.4.2 Requestors and Approving Officials are jointly responsible for ensuring sufficient funds are available prior to submission. Obligation of funds occurs upon approval of the production order.

5.4.3 Cost estimates are provided within 2 business days of request submission. The requestor must confirm acceptance of the estimate before production begins.

5.4.4 Requests exceeding the original estimate by more than 15% require re-approval at the appropriate threshold level.

### 5.5 Production Priorities

5.5.1 Requests are processed in the following priority order:
  1. **Mission-Critical** — Products directly supporting active operations, emergencies, or congressionally mandated deadlines
  2. **Rush** — 1–3 business day turnaround (50% surcharge applies)
  3. **Expedited** — 5–7 business day turnaround (25% surcharge applies)
  4. **Standard** — 10–15 business day turnaround (no surcharge)

5.5.2 The PDMC Director may re-prioritize the production queue based on agency operational needs. Affected requestors will be notified within 1 business day of any schedule changes.

### 5.6 Quality Assurance

5.6.1 All products undergo a minimum of two quality review cycles before delivery:
  - **Technical Review** — Conducted by PDMC production staff for print/production quality
  - **Content Review** — Conducted by the requestor for accuracy, completeness, and authorization

5.6.2 Requestors have 5 business days to complete their content review. Failure to respond within this window will result in the product being produced as-is.

5.6.3 Products requiring more than 3 revision cycles will incur an additional review surcharge of $75 per cycle.

### 5.7 Records Retention

5.7.1 All completed production orders and associated files are retained by the PDMC for a minimum of 6 years in accordance with FCA Directive 800.

5.7.2 Digital source files (.ai, .psd, .indd, etc.) are archived on the PDMC secure file server and may be retrieved for re-orders within the retention period.

5.7.3 Products containing CUI, FOUO, or Super Classified content are subject to additional retention and destruction requirements per FCA Directive 1200.

## 6. Responsibilities

### 6.1 Requestors shall:
- Submit accurate and complete requests through the authorized system
- Ensure valid funding is available before submission
- Respond to clarification requests within 5 business days
- Complete content review within the specified timeframe
- Comply with all classification, branding, and content policies

### 6.2 Approving Officials shall:
- Review and approve/deny requests within 3 business days
- Verify funding availability and appropriateness
- Ensure the requested product serves a legitimate government purpose
- Confirm classification markings are appropriate

### 6.3 PDMC shall:
- Process all compliant requests within established turnaround times
- Provide cost estimates within 2 business days
- Maintain quality standards for all produced materials
- Retain records in accordance with applicable directives
- Report monthly production metrics to the Administrative Services Division

## 7. Waivers

Waivers to any provision of this directive must be submitted in writing to the ASD Director. Waiver requests must include justification, risk assessment, and concurrence from the requestor's directorate leadership. Waivers are valid for a single request unless otherwise specified.

## 8. Effective Date

This directive is effective immediately upon publication and remains in effect until superseded or rescinded.
