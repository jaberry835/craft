# DIRECTIVE 1010 — Print Media Production Standards

**Issuing Authority:** Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC), Administrative Services Division
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2024
**Last Revised:** January 15, 2026
**Reference:** Directive 1000, Section 5

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the technical specifications, size limitations, material standards, quantity thresholds, and production requirements for all print media products requested through the PDMC. Compliance with these standards is mandatory for all requestors and ensures consistent quality, cost control, and regulatory adherence.

## 2. Scope

This directive applies to all print media sub-types processed by the PDMC, including:
- Posters
- Banners & Signage
- Copies & Reproduction
- Business Cards
- Brochures & Flyers
- Letterhead & Stationery
- Large Format Printing
- Booklets & Reports

---

## 3. Posters

### 3.1 Authorized Dimensions

| Size Designation | Dimensions (inches) | Max Quantity per Request | Notes |
|-----------------|---------------------|------------------------|-------|
| Small | 11" × 17" | 500 | Standard office poster |
| Medium | 18" × 24" | 250 | Hallway/common area display |
| Standard | 24" × 36" | 100 | Event and campaign posters |
| Large | 36" × 48" | 50 | Requires Division Chief approval |
| Oversized | 48" × 72" | 25 | Requires Directorate approval; see Sec. 3.3 |

3.1.1 Custom dimensions are permitted only when the requested size falls **between** two authorized sizes. Custom sizes may not exceed the Oversized maximum of 48" × 72".

3.1.2 Poster dimensions exceeding 36" × 48" are classified as **Large Format** and are subject to the requirements in Section 8 of this directive.

### 3.2 Material Requirements

3.2.1 Standard posters shall be printed on 100 lb. gloss or matte coated stock unless otherwise specified and approved.

3.2.2 Outdoor posters require UV-resistant lamination and must be noted as "outdoor use" in the request description. Outdoor posters incur a 30% material surcharge.

3.2.3 Posters intended for display periods exceeding 90 days must use archival-grade materials and specify the intended display duration in the description.

### 3.3 Content Requirements for Poster Requests

The request description for posters **must** include:
- (a) The intended display location(s) — building and area (e.g., "Building 401, Main Lobby")
- (b) The anticipated display duration (e.g., "30 days" or "permanent")
- (c) Whether the poster is for an event, campaign, informational display, or regulatory posting
- (d) The primary text/headline content or a reference to previously approved content
- (e) Whether photography or stock imagery is required (requestor must have usage rights)

Failure to include items (a) through (d) will result in the request being returned for clarification.

### 3.4 Quantity Restrictions

3.4.1 Poster requests exceeding 100 units require a written justification explaining the distribution plan. The justification must be included in the description or attached as a supporting document.

3.4.2 Single requests exceeding 500 posters of any size require Directorate Director approval regardless of cost.

3.4.3 Annual poster production per organizational element is capped at 2,000 units across all sizes. Requests that would exceed this cap must include a waiver request per Directive 1000, Section 7.

## 4. Banners & Signage

### 4.1 Authorized Dimensions

| Type | Dimensions | Max Quantity | Material |
|------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Retractable Banner | 33" × 80" | 10 per request | Vinyl with retractable stand |
| Tabletop Banner | 24" × 63" | 15 per request | Vinyl with tabletop stand |
| Yard Sign | 18" × 24" | 100 per request | Corrugated plastic (coroplast) |
| Vinyl Banner (Indoor) | Up to 4' × 8' | 5 per request | 13 oz. vinyl, hemmed edges |
| Vinyl Banner (Outdoor) | Up to 4' × 10' | 5 per request | 15 oz. vinyl, grommeted |
| Directional Signage | 12" × 18" | 50 per request | Rigid PVC or aluminum |

4.1.1 Banners exceeding 4' × 10' are considered **custom installations** and require a site survey by PDMC staff prior to production. A minimum 20-business-day lead time applies.

4.1.2 Retractable banner stands are agency property and must be returned to PDMC within 5 business days of event conclusion. Lost or damaged stands will be charged at replacement cost ($185 per stand).

### 4.2 Content Requirements for Banner Requests

The request description **must** include:
- (a) Event name and date(s), or permanent installation location
- (b) Whether hardware (stands, grommets, frames) is needed
- (c) Indoor or outdoor usage designation
- (d) Exact text content for the banner — banners with more than 50 words require Public Affairs review
- (e) For yard signs: a site map or description of placement locations

### 4.3 Special Restrictions

4.3.1 Banners and signage displayed in public-facing areas (lobbies, entrances, perimeter) **must** receive Public Affairs Office (PAO) approval before production. The PAO approval reference number must be included in the request.

4.3.2 Banners may not contain information classified above "Unclassified / Public." FOUO, CUI, and Super Classified content on banners is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Copies & Reproduction

### 5.1 Authorized Specifications

| Parameter | Standard | Maximum |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Paper Size | 8.5" × 11" (Letter) | 11" × 17" (Tabloid) |
| Quantity (B&W) | Up to 1,000 per request | 10,000 with Division Chief approval |
| Quantity (Color) | Up to 500 per request | 5,000 with Division Chief approval |
| Duplex | Available | — |
| Binding | Staple, 3-hole punch | Coil, comb, or perfect bind (see Booklets) |
| Paper Weight | 20 lb. bond | 28 lb. bond (cardstock covers available) |

5.1.1 Reproduction requests exceeding 1,000 color copies or 5,000 B&W copies are subject to the Government Publishing Office (GPO) referral requirements under 44 U.S.C. § 501. The PDMC will coordinate GPO referral when applicable.

5.1.2 Requests for reproduction of copyrighted material must include evidence of usage rights or a completed Copyright Compliance Certification (Form FCA-891).

### 5.2 Content Requirements for Copy Requests

The request description **must** include:
- (a) Whether the request is for new content or reproduction of existing material
- (b) If existing material: the document title, date, and version number
- (c) Single-sided or double-sided printing preference
- (d) Any finishing requirements (stapling, hole-punch, collating)
- (e) Total page count of the source document

### 5.3 Restrictions

5.3.1 The PDMC **will not** reproduce personal documents, unofficial materials, or documents for non-government purposes.

5.3.2 Documents marked FOUO or CUI must be reproduced using secure print procedures. The requestor must be present to receive the output, or designate a cleared recipient by name in the request.

5.3.3 Super Classified documents **may not** be reproduced through the PDMC. All Super Classified reproduction must be performed through the Security Directorate's Controlled Reproduction Facility (CRF).

## 6. Business Cards

### 6.1 Authorized Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Dimensions | 3.5" × 2" (standard US) — no exceptions |
| Paper Stock | 16 pt. coated matte or gloss, or 14 pt. uncoated |
| Color | Full color front; single-color or full-color back |
| Quantity | Minimum 100, Maximum 500 per individual per request |
| Layout | Must conform to FCA Directive 925 approved template |
| Re-order Interval | Minimum 90 days between orders for the same individual |

### 6.2 Content Requirements

6.2.1 Business cards **must** use the FCA-approved template. No custom layouts, fonts, or color schemes are permitted.

6.2.2 The following information is **required** on all business cards:
- Full legal name (as it appears in official personnel records)
- Official job title (as listed in the position description)
- Office symbol
- Official agency phone number (DSN and/or commercial)
- Official .gov email address
- Agency logo (placed per Directive 925 specifications)

6.2.3 The following information is **prohibited** on business cards:
- Personal cell phone numbers (unless authorized by Directorate Director in writing)
- Social media handles or personal websites
- Unofficial titles, nicknames, or credentials not recognized by the agency
- QR codes (unless approved by the IT Security Office)

6.2.4 Contractors are **not** authorized to receive FCA business cards unless operating in an official liaison capacity with written authorization from their Contracting Officer's Representative (COR).

### 6.3 Special Provisions

6.3.1 SES-level and Flag Officer business cards may use premium 32 pt. stock with embossed or foil elements. These cards are limited to 250 per order and require a separate cost justification.

6.3.2 Bilingual business cards are authorized for personnel in international liaison positions. The secondary language must be approved by the Foreign Affairs Directorate.

## 7. Brochures & Flyers

### 7.1 Authorized Dimensions

| Type | Flat Size | Finished Size | Max Quantity |
|------|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Single-Page Flyer | 8.5" × 11" | 8.5" × 11" | 2,000 |
| Half-Page Flyer | 8.5" × 5.5" | 8.5" × 5.5" | 3,000 |
| Bi-Fold Brochure | 11" × 8.5" | 5.5" × 8.5" | 1,000 |
| Tri-Fold Brochure | 11" × 8.5" (landscape) | 3.67" × 8.5" | 1,000 |
| Z-Fold Brochure | 11" × 8.5" (landscape) | 3.67" × 8.5" | 1,000 |
| Gate-Fold Brochure | 17" × 11" | 4.25" × 11" | 500 |
| Custom Fold | Varies | Varies | 500 — requires pre-production consult |

### 7.2 Content Requirements

The request description **must** include:
- (a) Whether content is final or requires PDMC design assistance
- (b) The fold type and finished size desired
- (c) The intended audience (internal staff, public, inter-agency)
- (d) Whether the brochure is for a specific event (include event name and date)
- (e) All text content or a content outline — brochures with incomplete text will not be designed
- (f) Any imagery requirements and confirmation of usage rights

### 7.3 Restrictions

7.3.1 Brochures intended for public distribution **must** include the agency disclaimer statement: *"This document is produced by the Federal Consolidated Agency for informational purposes. It does not constitute official policy or create any legal obligation."*

7.3.2 Brochures exceeding 1,000 copies trigger GPO referral review. The PDMC will notify the requestor if GPO production is required.

7.3.3 Brochures and flyers may not contain fundraising solicitations, political content, or endorsements of commercial products or services.

## 8. Large Format Printing

### 8.1 Authorized Specifications

| Parameter | Indoor | Outdoor |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Maximum Width | 60 inches | 60 inches |
| Maximum Length | 300 inches (25 feet) | 120 inches (10 feet) |
| Resolution | Minimum 150 DPI at print size | Minimum 100 DPI at print size |
| Material | Photo paper, canvas, adhesive vinyl, fabric | UV vinyl, mesh, aluminum composite |
| Lamination | Optional gloss or matte | Required — UV-resistant |

### 8.2 Content Requirements

The request description **must** include:
- (a) Exact finished dimensions (width × height in inches)
- (b) Mounting or finishing requirements (grommets, pole pockets, adhesive backing, foam board)
- (c) Indoor or outdoor installation designation
- (d) Installation location and surface type
- (e) Whether PDMC installation assistance is required (available for an additional fee)
- (f) Source file specifications (file format, resolution, color space)

8.2.1 Source files for large format printing **must** be provided in one of the following formats:
- Adobe Illustrator (.ai) with fonts outlined
- Adobe InDesign (.indd) with packaged fonts and links
- PDF/X-4 with embedded fonts
- High-resolution TIFF or PSD (minimum 150 DPI at 50% of print size)

8.2.2 Files submitted in PowerPoint, Word, or JPEG format **will be rejected** for large format production. The requestor will be asked to provide compliant file formats.

### 8.3 Approval Requirements

8.3.1 All large format requests exceeding 36" in any dimension require a pre-production consultation with PDMC staff. The requestor must schedule this consultation before submitting the formal request.

8.3.2 Large format installations in executive areas (Director suites, conference rooms designated "Executive" or above) require approval from the Facilities Management Office.

8.3.3 Permanent installations (intended display period exceeding 1 year) require Environmental and Safety Office review for fire code compliance.

## 9. Letterhead & Stationery

### 9.1 Authorized Products

| Product | Size | Stock | Quantity Limits |
|---------|------|-------|----------------|
| Letterhead | 8.5" × 11" | 24 lb. cotton bond | 500 – 5,000 per request |
| Envelopes (#10) | 4.125" × 9.5" | 24 lb. white wove | 500 – 5,000 per request |
| Envelopes (6×9) | 6" × 9" | 24 lb. white wove | 250 – 2,000 per request |
| Envelopes (9×12) | 9" × 12" | 28 lb. kraft or white | 100 – 1,000 per request |
| Notepads | 5.5" × 8.5" or 8.5" × 11" | 20 lb. bond, 50 sheets/pad | 25 – 200 pads per request |
| Memo Pads | 4.25" × 5.5" | 20 lb. bond, 50 sheets/pad | 50 – 500 pads per request |

### 9.2 Content Requirements

9.2.1 All letterhead and envelopes **must** use the FCA-approved masthead design per Directive 925. No custom mastheads are permitted.

9.2.2 Organizational letterhead (e.g., for a specific directorate) is authorized only for organizations at the Division level and above. Branch-level and below must use parent organization letterhead.

9.2.3 Personalized letterhead (with an individual's name and title) is authorized only for SES, Flag Officers, and office directors (GS-15 Division Chiefs and above).

### 9.3 Restrictions

9.3.1 Letterhead and stationery marked with any classification level above "Unclassified" is prohibited. Classified correspondence uses separate formats managed by the Security Directorate.

9.3.2 Maximum annual stationery expenditure per organizational element is $2,500. Requests that would exceed this cap require ASD Director approval.

## 10. Booklets & Reports

### 10.1 Authorized Specifications

| Parameter | Standard | Premium |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Page Size | 8.5" × 11" or 5.5" × 8.5" | 8.5" × 11" or custom (with approval) |
| Page Count | 4 – 96 pages (including covers) | Up to 200 pages |
| Binding | Saddle-stitch (up to 64 pages) | Perfect bind, wire-o, or coil |
| Cover Stock | 80 lb. gloss or matte coated | 100 lb. coated or laminated |
| Interior Stock | 80 lb. gloss text | 100 lb. silk or matte text |
| Color | Full color or B&W | Full color |
| Max Quantity | 500 per request | 250 per request |

### 10.2 Content Requirements

The request description **must** include:
- (a) Total page count (must be a multiple of 4 for saddle-stitch binding)
- (b) Binding type preference
- (c) Whether a table of contents and/or index is required
- (d) Cover design requirements or reference to existing design
- (e) Complete content in final form — the PDMC does not perform content editing on booklets
- (f) Whether the booklet contains any controlled or limited distribution content

### 10.3 GPO Referral

10.3.1 Booklet and report production exceeding 200 copies **or** exceeding 96 pages is automatically referred to the Government Publishing Office for production determination under 44 U.S.C. § 501.

10.3.2 The PDMC will coordinate with GPO on the requestor's behalf. Additional lead time of 15–30 business days may apply for GPO-produced materials.

## 11. General Print Production Rules

### 11.1 Color Accuracy

11.1.1 All agency materials must use approved brand colors as specified in Directive 925. The approved Pantone Matching System (PMS) colors are:
- **Primary:** PMS 2758 (Agency Blue), PMS 7546 (Agency Gray)
- **Secondary:** PMS 7409 (Agency Gold), PMS 349 (Agency Green)
- **Accent:** PMS 1797 (Alert Red) — restricted to warning/safety materials only

11.1.2 CMYK equivalents must be used for 4-color process printing. RGB files will be converted and may result in color shifts for which the PDMC is not responsible.

### 11.2 Proofing

11.2.1 A digital proof will be provided for all print orders exceeding $250 in estimated cost. The requestor must approve the proof before production proceeds.

11.2.2 Hard copy (press) proofs are available upon request for orders exceeding $2,500. Hard copy proofs incur a $50 fee and add 2 business days to the production timeline.

11.2.3 Proof approval constitutes acceptance of the design. Errors discovered after proof approval that are attributable to the requestor's content will incur reprint charges.

### 11.3 Sustainability

11.3.1 In accordance with Executive Order 14057 (Federal Sustainability Plan), all standard print stocks must contain a minimum of 30% post-consumer recycled content.

11.3.2 Requests for non-recycled or specialty stocks must include an environmental justification and are subject to approval by the Sustainability Officer.
