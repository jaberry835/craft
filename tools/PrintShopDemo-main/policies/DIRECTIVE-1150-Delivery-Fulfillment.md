# DIRECTIVE 1150 — Delivery, Fulfillment & Turnaround Standards

**Issuing Authority:** Print & Digital Media Center (PDMC), Logistics & Fulfillment Branch
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2024
**Last Revised:** March 30, 2026
**Reference:** Directive 1000, Sections 3 and 7; Directive 1200, Section 2.4

---

## 1. Purpose

This directive establishes the delivery methods, fulfillment procedures, turnaround timelines, shipping restrictions, and completion standards for all print and digital media products produced by the Print & Digital Media Center. Proper fulfillment ensures that products reach requestors securely, on time, and in condition suitable for their intended purpose.

## 2. Production Turnaround Standards

### 2.2 Timeline Adjustments

2.2.1 **Revision Cycles:** Each revision cycle adds to the production timeline:
- Standard priority: +2 BD per revision cycle
- Expedited priority: +1 BD per revision cycle
- Rush priority: +1 BD per revision cycle (beginning second revision)

2.2.2 **Security Review:** Products requiring manual security review (per Directive 1200, Section 4) add 2-5 BD.

2.2.3 **PAO Review:** Products requiring Public Affairs Office review (per Directive 1100, Section 5.3) add 3-5 BD.

2.2.4 **Legal Review:** Products requiring OGC review (per Directive 1100, Section 5.4) add 5-10 BD.

2.2.5 Cumulative timeline adjustments are additive. A product requiring both security and PAO review at standard priority may take up to 25 BD total.

### 2.3 Rush Availability Restrictions

2.3.1 Rush priority is **not available** for:
- Products with estimated costs exceeding $5,000 (per Directive 1050, Section 3.1.2)
- Logo design (requires BSB approval process that cannot be expedited)
- Letterhead and envelopes (requires precise color matching and quality control)
- Booklets (complex production with binding and finishing)
- Video and motion graphics exceeding 30 seconds
- Products requiring Super Classified handling
- Presentations exceeding 30 slides

2.3.2 The PDMC reserves the right to decline Rush priority during periods of high production volume. The requestor will be notified within 4 business hours of submission if Rush cannot be accommodated.

2.3.3 Only **two (2) Rush requests per organization per calendar week** are accepted. Additional Rush requests within the same week require Deputy Director-level justification.

## 3. Delivery Methods

### 3.1 Available Delivery Methods

| Method | Description | Availability |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Office Pickup | Requestor picks up from PDMC, Building 1, Suite 200 | All products |
| Internal Mail | Delivered via FCA internal mail service | Print products only, Unclassified/FOUO only |
| Courier Delivery | Hand-delivered by PDMC courier to specified office | All print products, all classification levels |
| Digital Delivery | Electronic file delivery via secure system | Digital products only |

### 3.2 Office Pickup

3.2.1 The PDMC customer service window is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM ET.

3.2.2 Products are held for pickup for **10 business days** after completion notification. After 10 business days, the requestor receives a final reminder. Products not picked up within **15 business days** are archived (digital) or recycled (print), and the full cost is still charged.

3.2.3 Pickup requires presentation of a government-issued photo ID and the PDMC order confirmation number.

3.2.4 Designated alternates may pick up products if the requestor has submitted an Authorized Pickup Designee form (FCA-775) listing the alternate by name.

### 3.3 Internal Mail Delivery

3.3.1 Internal mail delivery is available for **Unclassified and FOUO** print products only.

3.3.2 Products must be addressed to a valid FCA internal mail stop. Personal delivery addresses are not accepted.

3.3.3 Internal mail delivery adds **2-3 business days** to the delivery timeline beyond the production completion date.

3.3.4 Maximum weight for internal mail delivery: **15 pounds** per package. Products exceeding this weight require Courier Delivery.

3.3.5 The PDMC is not responsible for damage to products during internal mail transit. For high-value or fragile products (e.g., mounted large-format prints, framed items), Courier Delivery is strongly recommended.

3.3.6 Internal mail delivery is **not available** for:
- CUI products (require courier with signature)
- Super Classified products (require secure courier)
- Products requiring assembly or installation (banners, multi-panel displays)
- Products exceeding 15 pounds per package

### 3.4 Courier Delivery

3.4.1 PDMC courier service operates Monday through Friday, with delivery runs at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM ET.

3.4.2 Courier delivery is required for:
- All CUI products (courier obtains recipient signature)
- All Super Classified products (courier has SC clearance and follows chain-of-custody protocol)
- Products exceeding 15 pounds
- Mounted, framed, or oversized products
- Products requiring installation or setup

3.4.3 **CUI Courier Protocol:**
- Products are sealed in opaque, tamper-evident packaging
- Courier maintains a chain-of-custody form (FCA-CC-001)
- Recipient must present government photo ID and sign the custody form
- If recipient is unavailable, the product is **returned to the PDMC** — it will not be left with alternates unless pre-authorized

3.4.4 **Super Classified Courier Protocol:**
- Products are sealed in double-wrapped, tamper-evident packaging with numbered security seals
- Courier is a PDMC employee with active Super Classified access authorization
- Courier does not make any other stops between PDMC and the delivery destination
- Two-person integrity is required — courier is accompanied by a second authorized individual
- Recipient must verify security seal numbers against the advance notification before accepting
- Delivery is limited to Buildings 1, 3, and 7 (Super Classified-authorized facilities)
- Delivery to off-campus locations is **not authorized** for Super Classified products

3.4.5 Courier delivery coverage area:
- **FCA Main Campus** (Buildings 1-12): Standard courier service
- **FCA Annex Complex** (Annexes A-E): Courier available, +1 BD to timeline
- **FCA Regional Offices** (within 50-mile radius): Courier available by appointment, +2 BD to timeline
- **FCA Regional Offices** (beyond 50-mile radius): Not available — use approved shipping service

### 3.5 Digital Delivery

3.5.1 Digital products are delivered electronically via the PDMC Secure File Transfer portal.

3.5.2 The requestor receives an email notification with a secure download link when the product is ready. Links expire after **30 calendar days**.

3.5.3 File size limits:
- Standard download: Up to 500 MB per delivery
- Large file delivery (over 500 MB): Arranged through the PDMC Project Manager via alternative secure transfer

3.5.4 Digital delivery formats:
| Classification | Delivery Method | Encryption |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| Unclassified | PDMC Secure Portal (standard) | TLS in transit |
| FOUO | PDMC Secure Portal (authenticated) | TLS in transit, access logging |
| CUI | PDMC Secure Portal (CUI-accredited) | AES-256 encrypted files, access logging, download audit |
| Super Classified | SC-accredited system only (not via portal) | AES-256, hardware token authentication, one-time download |

3.5.5 Super Classified digital products may **not** be delivered via email, the standard secure portal, or any cloud-based file sharing service. Delivery is restricted to the SC-accredited file transfer system or physical encrypted media (see Section 3.6).

### 3.6 Physical Media Delivery (Digital Products)

3.6.1 Requestors may request delivery of digital products on physical media when:
- Files are too large for electronic transfer
- The receiving system does not have network access to the PDMC portal
- Classification requirements mandate physical media

3.6.2 Physical media options:
| Media Type | Capacity | Cost |
|-----------|----------|------|
| USB flash drive (encrypted) | Up to 64 GB | $15 per drive |
| External hard drive (encrypted) | Up to 2 TB | $85 per drive |
| DVD-R | Up to 4.7 GB | $5 per disc |
| Blu-ray disc | Up to 25 GB | $10 per disc |

3.6.3 Physical media for CUI or Super Classified products must be agency-approved encrypted devices registered with IT Security. Personal or commercially purchased USB drives are not accepted.

## 4. Shipping Standards (External)

### 4.1 Authorized Shipping Services

4.1.1 For products that must be shipped outside the FCA campus area, the following carriers are authorized:
- U.S. Postal Service (USPS) — Unclassified products only
- FedEx (government account) — Unclassified and FOUO products
- UPS (government account) — Unclassified and FOUO products
- GSA Scheduled Delivery Service — All classification levels up to CUI

4.1.2 **Super Classified products may not be shipped via any commercial carrier.** Delivery is restricted to PDMC courier or authorized government transport.

### 4.2 Shipping Cost Responsibility

4.2.1 Shipping costs are charged to the requestor's fund cite in addition to the production cost.

4.2.2 The requestor selects the shipping speed; the PDMC arranges the shipment.

### 4.3 International Shipping

4.3.1 Shipment of media products to locations outside the United States is generally **not authorized** through the PDMC.

4.3.2 Exceptions may be granted by the Deputy Director for products supporting official agency activities at foreign posts, embassies, or international conferences. The requestor must provide a signed International Shipping Authorization (FCA-ISA-100).

4.3.3 Products containing FOUO, CUI, or Super Classified information may **never** be shipped internationally.

## 5. Completion & Acceptance

### 5.1 Completion Notification

5.1.1 The PDMC sends a completion notification to the requestor via email and the PDMC tracking portal when the product is ready for delivery or pickup.

5.1.2 The notification includes:
- Order number and product description
- Delivery method and estimated delivery date
- Final cost (may differ from estimate due to actuals)
- Quality inspection result (pass/fail/notes)

### 5.2 Quality Inspection

5.2.1 All print products undergo a final quality inspection before release, checking:
- Color accuracy (within Delta E 3.0 of proof)
- Print registration and alignment
- Trim accuracy (within 1/16" tolerance)
- Binding integrity and page order
- Finishing quality (lamination, mounting, folding)
- Correct quantity

5.2.2 Products failing quality inspection are reprinted at no additional cost to the requestor.

### 5.3 Acceptance & Dispute

5.3.1 The requestor has **5 business days** from delivery to report quality issues or defects.

5.3.2 Defect reports must include:
- Order number
- Description of the defect
- Photographs of the defect (for print products)
- Quantity affected

5.3.3 Valid defects identified within the 5 BD window are corrected or reprinted at no additional cost. Defects reported after 5 BD are handled on a case-by-case basis and may incur reprint charges.

## 6. Request Holds & Cancellations

### 6.1 Administrative Hold

6.1.1 Requests are placed on Administrative Hold when:
- The requestor fails to respond to a proof within 10 business days
- The fund cite is invalid or has insufficient funds
- Required approvals are missing or expired
- Security review flags unresolved issues

6.1.2 Requests on Administrative Hold for **30 calendar days** are automatically cancelled (per Directive 1000, Section 7.1).

### 6.2 Requestor-Initiated Cancellation

6.2.1 Requestors may cancel requests at any time by contacting the PDMC Project Manager or through the PDMC tracking portal.

6.2.2 Cancellation charges apply per Directive 1050, Section 6.3:
- Before production: No charge
- After production starts, before first proof: 25%
- After first proof: 50%
- After final proof approval: 100%

### 6.3 PDMC-Initiated Cancellation

6.3.1 The PDMC may cancel a request when:
- The request violates policy that cannot be remediated
- The requestor is unresponsive for 30 calendar days
- The fund cite is permanently invalid
- Security review determines the product cannot be produced at the requested classification level

6.3.2 The PDMC provides written notice to the requestor and their supervisor before PDMC-initiated cancellation.

## 7. Reorders & Reprints

### 7.1 Reorder Eligibility

7.1.1 Products may be reordered within **12 months** of the original production date using a simplified reorder process (no design phase).

7.1.2 Reorders must reference the original order number. The PDMC retains production files for 12 months after completion.

7.1.3 Reorders of identical products are charged at **material and quantity cost only** (no base design cost).

### 7.2 Reprint Due to PDMC Error

7.2.1 If a product defect is attributable to a PDMC production error (not requestor-provided content), the product is reprinted at no cost.

7.2.2 PDMC errors include: incorrect color reproduction, wrong paper stock, binding failure, misalignment, wrong quantity.

7.2.3 Content errors (typos, wrong information, incorrect names) that were present in the requestor-approved proof are **not** PDMC errors and are subject to standard reprint charges.
